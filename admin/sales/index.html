The Story to Tell Your Prospect

Ask any sales manager what their biggest frustration is and they'll tell you the same thing: their reps aren't selling enough. But when you break down a typical sales rep's day, the problem isn't effort — it's time allocation. On average, a sales rep spends only 35% of their working day actually selling. The rest? Admin. CRM updates. Writing emails. Chasing leads. Scheduling meetings.

"You're paying a sales salary to do admin. That's the problem. AI fixes the admin."

The Volume Problem

The most reliable predictor of sales success is volume. More conversations lead to more conversions. It's almost mathematical. A rep who contacts 30 prospects a day will always outperform one who contacts 15 — assuming equal close rates.

But physically, a human can only contact so many people. Writing a personalised email takes time. Researching a prospect takes time. Logging the interaction in the CRM takes time. By the time they've done all of that, the day is over and they've sent 25 emails.

An AI-powered outreach system operates on a completely different scale. With the right setup, your team can send 200 to 500 highly personalised, relevant emails per day — each one tailored to the specific recipient's role, company, and context. That's not spam. That's targeted, intelligent outreach at a scale no human could maintain.

Analogy to use: "Imagine hiring 10 sales reps — but none of them need a salary, a desk, or a holiday. That's roughly what AI outreach does to your pipeline capacity. Your existing reps then handle the conversations, not the prospecting."

The Follow-Up Problem

Research consistently shows that 80% of sales happen after 5 or more follow-ups. Yet most sales reps give up after one or two attempts. Why? Because manually tracking and scheduling follow-ups across 200 prospects is overwhelming.

AI automation sequences solve this completely. The moment a prospect is added to the system, they enter a follow-up sequence — day 3, day 7, day 14 — each with a different angle, a different subject line, a different reason to engage. Nothing is forgotten. Nobody falls through the cracks. Ever.

The Financial Picture

A sales rep on £40,000 a year is costing you roughly £19 an hour. If they're spending 2.5 hours per day on admin that AI can handle, that's approximately £50 a day — over £12,000 a year — of selling time being spent on data entry and email writing.

But the bigger number isn't the cost saving. It's the revenue uplift. When your rep's pipeline goes from 30 prospects a day to 300, even if the close rate stays the same, you've just 10× your pipeline. That's not a 10% improvement. That's business transformation.

"We don't just save you money on admin. We give your sales team a superpower they've never had before — the ability to be everywhere at once."

What to Emphasise

  • Volume: 10× more outreach with the same team — no new hires needed.
  • Personalisation: AI research means every email is relevant, not generic — higher reply rates.
  • Consistency: Automated follow-ups mean no prospect is ever forgotten or left cold.
  • Data quality: CRM stays clean and current automatically — no stale data or missed entries.
  • Rep performance: When admin is gone, reps spend their day on what they're actually good at — closing.

Objection Handlers
"Won't mass outreach damage our brand?" +

This confuses automation with spam. Our system doesn't send generic blasts — it sends highly relevant, personalised messages to people who are genuinely good fits for your product. When an email is relevant and well-written, people don't think "this is automated" — they think "this company actually understands my situation."

"Our sales process is too complex for automation" +

Automation doesn't replace the complex parts — it handles the simple, repetitive parts that lead up to them. Finding leads, writing first-touch emails, sending follow-ups, logging in the CRM — none of that requires complexity. It requires consistency. AI provides that, so your reps can focus their expertise on the conversations that actually need it.

"We already have a CRM — why do we need this?" +

A CRM is a place to store data. AI automation is a system that generates, acts on, and updates that data — automatically. Most CRMs are only as good as the data in them, and most data is entered manually, infrequently, and inaccurately. We make your CRM actually work.
