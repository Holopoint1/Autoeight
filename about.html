About Autoeight

Autoeight is a web design, automation and data agency based in Halifax, West Yorkshire, United Kingdom. We help growing UK businesses with four services under one roof: web design & SEO, AI & sales automation, CRM & integration, and learning management systems. The websites get found on search engines and AI search, the integrated AI and automation solutions take hours of manual work from your team, and the dashboards turn raw data into decisions that give you advantage. Everything is built to work as one connected system.

We've built websites and systems for clients across construction, education, retail, e-commerce, and professional services. Every site is custom-built (no templates), optimised for Google and AI search, mobile-first, and connected to the business tools our clients already use. Our differentiator is that we don't just hand over a website and walk away. We integrate it with your CRM, wire up automated lead capture, build the reporting layer that shows you what's working, and keep it all running long after launch.
