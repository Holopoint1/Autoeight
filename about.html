About Autoeight

Autoeight is a web design agency based in Halifax, West Yorkshire, United Kingdom. Founded by Adam Sheridan, the company builds conversion-focused business websites for growing UK companies. Autoeight specialises in custom web design, AI integration, CRM connections, and sales automation built directly into every website we deliver.

We've built websites for clients across construction, education, retail, e-commerce, and professional services. Every site is custom-built (no templates), optimised for Google search, mobile-first, and connected to the business tools our clients already use. Our differentiator is that we don't just hand over a website and walk away. We integrate it with your CRM, set up automated lead capture, and keep it running long after launch.

Why Automation?

See the difference automation makes

Most businesses are still doing everything by hand. Researching prospects, writing emails one at a time, chasing follow-ups, updating spreadsheets. It's slow, it's tiring, and it doesn't scale. Here's what it looks like when you stop doing all that manually.

Without Automation
Finding Prospects

Your team manually searches for leads, Googles each one individually, and copies their details into a spreadsheet. That's 2 hours gone before anyone's even sent a message.

⏲ 2 hrs wasted / day
Outreach

Every email gets written from scratch. LinkedIn messages go out one by one. There's no personalisation because there's no time for it. Generic messages get generic results.

⏲ 3 hrs wasted / day
Follow-ups

People forget to follow up. Leads go cold after 48 hours. The CRM doesn't get updated. Nobody knows what's been sent to who. Deals slip through the cracks.

⚠ Revenue lost
5+ hours wasted per person, per day
With Automation
Finding Prospects

AI finds your ideal prospects automatically. It pulls data from LinkedIn, the web, and social profiles, then builds a full prospect profile for each one. No manual searching.
