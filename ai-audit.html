The lens

Where we look for AI wins.

Every audit runs through the same five lenses. Different businesses have different problems, but the questions are consistent.

Processes

Where the hours actually go

Which repeated tasks are eating your team's week. Quote writing, follow-up, invoicing, data entry, reporting, prep for calls, chasing suppliers. Everything that's manual but shouldn't be.

Tech stack

How your tools talk (or don't)

Every SME has a stack of tools that grew by accident. We map it, look for the gaps between them, and identify where AI plus integration would remove 80% of the copy-pasting.

Communication

Inbox, sales, support flows

Emails you keep rewriting, replies you keep drafting, follow-ups that get missed, support tickets that need triaging. Where an AI assistant would take 10 hours off a week without anyone noticing.

Data & reporting

What you can't see today

Every business has data. Most can't see it. We look at what dashboards you'd want, where the numbers live now, and how AI can pull them into decisions your team can act on daily.

Sales & customers

The moments that matter

Lead capture, qualification, conversion, retention, review generation. Every touchpoint where AI can help you respond faster, personalise better, or close more than you're closing now.

Honest limits

Where AI won't help

Not everything is an AI problem. We'll tell you what's better solved by a hire, a process change, or better software before we recommend AI. If AI isn't the answer, we say so.

FAQs

Common questions about the AI Audit.

What is an AI audit, exactly?

A structured one-hour session where we walk through your business, tech stack and processes to identify specific ways AI and automation would save you time, cost or competitive ground. You get a written report within five business days with prioritised recommendations, rough ROI estimates and tool suggestions.

Who is this for?

UK small and medium-sized businesses that suspect AI could help but don't know where to start. Best fit is 10 to 49 employees, running a mix of manual admin, disconnected tools and processes that grew organically. Not aimed at teams already running AI heavily or at enterprises with in-house AI functions.

How much does it cost?

£250, paid upfront when you book. That covers the one-hour session with a Founder-led consultant and the written report delivered within five business days.

How long does the audit take?

The live session is up to one hour on Google Meet. The written report is delivered within five business days.

What if my team is worried about AI replacing jobs?

Fair concern. In practice, most of what we identify is manual admin work no-one enjoys anyway: data entry, chasing follow-ups, summarising emails, hunting through files. The audit report is honest about what AI can and can't do, and what would still need a human. You can share it with your team openly.

Do you sign an NDA?

Yes, on request. We'll sign a mutual NDA before the audit call if you want one. Just tell us when you book.

What tools do you look at?

Whatever you're using. CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho), comms (Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Teams), storage (Google Drive, SharePoint, Dropbox), project management (Notion, Asana, Trello), sales (LinkedIn, Apollo, cold email tools) and any legacy systems. The report will tell you where AI plugs in and which combinations pay back fastest.

What if I could just do the AI work myself?

You probably could. The audit tells you which opportunities are worth doing yourself, which are worth outsourcing to a freelancer, and which are worth Autoeight building end to end. Not every recommendation ends in us quoting you for a build.
