FAQ

The questions everyone asks before they reach out.

How long does a typical build take?

Anywhere from 2 to 12 weeks depending on scope. A focused build like an automated quote-request system or a chase-sequence engine is usually 2-4 weeks. We give you a fixed timeline before we start so you're not guessing.

How much does this cost?

Custom builds typically run from £2,000 for a focused automation up to £25,000+ for a full multi-module platform. We quote per-project, fixed price, no hidden hourly billing. The 30-minute audit is free and you'll get a rough budget by the end of the call so you know where we stand.

Who owns the code when you're done?

You do. Full source code, your repo, your hosting, your data. We're not in the business of holding your software hostage. If you want to take it elsewhere later or hire a different team, you can. Your business shouldn't be at the mercy of any single vendor, including us.

Do you work with non-UK businesses?

Yes. We're based in Halifax and most clients are UK-based, but a chunk of our work runs remotely with businesses across the EU and US. The work is the same. Time zones just mean async-first communication and a slightly tighter weekly review cadence.

What if I have a vague idea and don't know what I actually need?

That's most of our clients. The 30-minute audit is built for this. You explain the pain, we ask the right questions, and by the end you'll have a clearer view of what the actual problem is and what would solve it. No commitment to build anything.

Do you charge for ongoing support after launch?

Bug fixes and minor tweaks for the first 30 days are included. After that, you can either bring it in-house, hire elsewhere, or keep us on a small retainer for ongoing changes. Most clients pick the retainer for the first few months and then go independent once their own team is up to speed.
