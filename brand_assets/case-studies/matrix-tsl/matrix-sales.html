The Problem

Matrix TSL is a UK-based manufacturer of engineering education equipment, supplying universities and colleges nationwide. The sales operation was underperforming — not because the team lacked ability, but because their workflow was fragmented. Each rep was spending over three hours a day switching between HubSpot, LinkedIn, spreadsheets, and manual web searches just to identify prospects, track deals, and prepare outreach.

There was no unified pipeline view. University prospecting was entirely manual — finding the right contact at the right department meant hours of searching across staff directories. Industry signals like new funding or course launches were being missed entirely because nobody had the capacity to monitor them. The business needed a way to give each rep their time back and turn scattered data into a structured sales process.

What We Did

We designed and built a single AI-powered sales intelligence platform that replaced every disconnected tool the team was using. From day one, the entire sales workflow — prospecting, pipeline management, outreach, and reporting — ran from one login.

AI Product Assistant

Instant product knowledge on demand

The platform includes an AI assistant trained on the full product catalogue. The team can query it in natural language — matching products to specific course requirements in seconds instead of manually searching catalogues. It provides talking points, specifications, and relevant use cases, cutting meeting preparation time significantly.

Live Pipeline

Real-time visibility across every deal

A live HubSpot integration pulls all deal stages, forecasts, and activity into the platform automatically. The team gets a single view of the entire pipeline without switching applications — improving forecast accuracy and ensuring no deal falls through the cracks.

Contact Discovery

Automated university prospecting

The platform automatically discovers engineering department contacts across target universities — sorted by discipline with direct contact details. What previously took hours of manual research per institution now takes seconds, allowing the team to focus on outreach rather than data entry.

Lead Intelligence

30+ industry feeds monitored daily

Over 30 news feeds from education, engineering, and renewables sectors are monitored and scored by relevance automatically. When a college receives funding or announces a new programme, the team sees the opportunity that morning — before competitors do.

The Result

The transformation was immediate. The sales team now operates from a single platform that handles prospecting, pipeline management, product queries, outreach, and reporting. Each rep recovered over three hours per day previously lost to manual research and tool-switching. Lead sources that were never monitored are now tracked automatically. University prospecting that used to take an entire morning is completed in minutes. The business moved from a fragmented, reactive sales process to a structured, intelligence-led operation.
