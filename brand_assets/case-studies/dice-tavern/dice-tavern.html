The Problem

Harry runs The Dice Tavern from York — he laser-cuts MDF terrain for Warhammer 40K players. Tournament organisers, gaming clubs, and hobbyists buy his terrain sets, gaming mats, and storage solutions. Business was growing, but he was selling everything through Etsy, which meant Etsy took a cut of every sale, controlled the customer experience, and made it hard to build a direct relationship with buyers.

He needed his own store — one that handled payments, sent order updates automatically, showed customers the right products, and gave him a dashboard to manage orders, stock, emails, and reviews without needing to be a developer.

What We Did

We built a complete online store from scratch — not a Shopify theme, not a WordPress plugin. A fully custom platform designed around how Harry actually runs his business.

Storefront

A shop that sells itself

Product pages with image galleries, stock indicators, trust badges (Bestseller, Popular, Low Stock), star ratings, and a smart recommendation engine that suggests related products based on what you're looking at. Customers filter by category, price, and sort order. The whole thing feels like a premium shopping experience.

Payments

Stripe checkout, no middleman

Customers pay directly with Stripe — no Etsy fees, no marketplace cut. The checkout calculates postage automatically, applies discount codes, and handles the full payment flow. Harry keeps more of every sale.

Email Automation

4 emails sent automatically per order

When someone orders, they get an instant confirmation. When Harry marks it as dispatched, they get tracking info. 5 days after delivery, they get a follow-up. 14 days later, they get a review request with a discount code for their next purchase. All automatic — Harry doesn't touch any of it.

Admin

One dashboard to run everything

Harry manages products, orders, stock levels, discount codes, email templates, reviews, customer enquiries, and site settings — all from a single admin panel. He can see revenue at a glance, track orders through a 7-stage pipeline, and get alerts when stock runs low or something needs attention.

Smart Selling

Cross-sell popups that boost order value

When a customer adds a terrain set to their cart, a popup suggests a matching gaming mat at 10–15% off. Harry sets the rules — which products trigger which suggestions — and the system handles the rest. It's like having a salesperson who never forgets to upsell.

Analytics

See where every visitor comes from

Built-in traffic analytics show which countries visitors come from, what device they're on, which pages they visit, and where they found the site. Harry can see what's working without needing Google Analytics or any third-party tool.

The Result

Harry now runs his entire business from one platform. Orders come in through his own website — not Etsy — so he keeps more margin on every sale. Customers get professional, timely emails at every stage without Harry lifting a finger. The admin dashboard gives him a complete view of his business: revenue, orders, stock, reviews, and traffic. And when he wants to run a promotion or suggest related products, he sets it up himself in the dashboard — no developer needed.
