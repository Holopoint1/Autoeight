The Problem

The Dice Tavern sells custom-made Warhammer 40K terrain to a dedicated hobbyist market. The business was growing, but every sale ran through a third-party marketplace. That meant a cut of every transaction went to the platform, the marketplace owned the customer relationship, and there was no way to contact buyers after they'd bought something.

There were no follow-up emails, no way to encourage repeat purchases, no cross-selling, and no dashboard for managing orders or stock. Every sale was basically a one-off. Someone bought, and that was it. No way to bring them back.

What We Did

We built a custom e-commerce platform from scratch. The goal was simple: own the customer relationship, ditch the marketplace fees, and automate all the stuff that was being done by hand.

Direct Payments

Zero marketplace fees on every sale

We set up Stripe so payments go directly to the business. The checkout handles postage, discount codes, and the whole payment flow with no middleman taking a cut. Every sale goes straight to them now. Margins improved on every single transaction.

Email Automation

4 emails per order, all automatic

Every order triggers a sequence: instant confirmation, dispatch notification with tracking, a follow-up 5 days after delivery, and a review request with a return incentive at 14 days. The whole thing runs without anyone having to lift a finger.

Cross-Sell Engine

Smart product recommendations

At checkout, the platform suggests complementary products based on what's in the basket. The business controls the rules, which products trigger which recommendations, and sets up bundled discounts. Average order value goes up without any extra work.

Operations Dashboard

Full business visibility from one screen

Orders, stock levels, revenue, customer comms, reviews, and site analytics all live in one dashboard. The team can process orders through a multi-stage pipeline, run promotions, and check how things are going without switching between tools or relying on the marketplace for numbers.

The Result

The business runs entirely from its own platform now. They own the customer relationship. Marketplace fees are gone, so margins are better on every sale. The automated email sequence keeps a 4.9-star rating going through consistent, professional follow-ups, and the team doesn't have to do any of it manually. Four emails go out per order, driving reviews, repeat purchases, and loyalty. And the dashboard gives them full visibility over orders, stock, revenue, and customer data, all in one place instead of scattered across half a dozen tools.
