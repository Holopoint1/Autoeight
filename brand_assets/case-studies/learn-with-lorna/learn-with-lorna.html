The Problem

Lorna is a GCSE Maths and Geography tutor in Halifax. She's also an AQA Approved Examiner — so she knows exactly what students need to pass. But her business was running on duct tape. Assignments went out by email. Progress was tracked in a spreadsheet. Parents got updates on WhatsApp. Her website was a basic Wix page that just had a contact form.

She looked at off-the-shelf platforms like TutorCruncher and MyTutor, but they were expensive, bloated, and designed for big tutoring agencies — not a one-person business. She needed something simple, custom, and hers.

What We Did

We replaced every scattered tool with one custom platform. A professional website to bring in new students, and a backend that runs the whole business.

Website

A site that actually brings in students

We built a warm, professional website with real student testimonials, a clear explanation of how tutoring works, and a contact form that goes straight to Lorna's inbox and her database at the same time. Parents land on the page and know exactly what they're getting.

Students

Students log in and see their work

Each student has their own login. They see what work has been set, submit their answers, and check their grades and feedback. No more "did you get my email?" — everything is in one place.

Parents

Parents check progress anytime

Parents log in and see how their child is doing — grades, what's been submitted, what's outstanding. If they have more than one child with Lorna, they can switch between them. No more WhatsApp chasing.

Lorna

Everything managed from one screen

Lorna creates assignments, uploads worksheets, reviews submitted work, gives grades and feedback, manages all her students and parents, and handles new enquiries — all from a single admin dashboard. One login, everything sorted.

The Result

Lorna's tutoring business now runs from one platform. Students know where their work is. Parents can check in without asking. And Lorna spends her time actually teaching — not chasing emails, updating spreadsheets, or sending WhatsApp messages. The platform costs her nothing per month to run, and she owns every part of it.
