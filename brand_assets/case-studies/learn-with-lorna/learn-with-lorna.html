The Problem

Learn With Lorna is an online education provider offering GCSE tutoring services. The business had strong demand, but operations were fragmented across 5+ disconnected tools. Assignments went out by email. Progress was tracked in spreadsheets. Parent updates went through WhatsApp. Enquiries came through a basic form with no structured follow-up. Every week, hours of productive time were lost to manual admin that added no value to the service.

The business looked at off-the-shelf learning management systems, but they were built for large institutions — expensive (£200+/month), bloated with features that weren't needed, and rigid in how they worked. What the business actually needed was a simple, integrated system where students access their work, parents see progress, and the team manages everything from one place — without paying for software designed for someone else.

What We Did

We built a fully integrated learning management system — a single platform that handles everything the business needs. A professional website to attract new students, a student portal for accessing and submitting work, a parent dashboard for tracking progress, and an admin panel for managing the entire operation. Think of it as a custom-built version of what platforms like Google Classroom or TutorCruncher try to do — but designed specifically for this business, with no monthly fees and no features it doesn't need.

Lead Generation

A website that converts enquiries

We built a professional, SEO-optimised website with testimonials, clear service descriptions, and an integrated enquiry form that feeds directly into the management dashboard. Every new lead is captured, tracked, and followed up — with no manual data entry required.

Client Portal

Self-service access for every client

Each client has a secure login with visibility over their assignments, submissions, grades, and feedback. Progress updates that previously required manual emails and messages are now available on demand — reducing inbound queries and freeing up operational time.

Stakeholder View

Real-time progress visibility

A dedicated stakeholder dashboard provides real-time oversight of client progress — grades, outstanding work, and submission history — all without a single phone call or message. For clients with multiple accounts, everything is accessible from one login.

Operations Dashboard

Complete business management in one place

Assignment creation, content distribution, submission review, grading, client management, and enquiry handling — all consolidated into a single admin dashboard. The entire operational workflow runs from one screen, eliminating the need for spreadsheets, email chains, and third-party platforms.

The Result

The learning management system transformed how the business operates. Admin time dropped by 60% — the hours previously spent emailing assignments, updating spreadsheets, and chasing parents for responses simply don't exist anymore. Students log in, see their work, submit it, and get feedback — all in one place. Parents check progress whenever they want without having to ask. The team manages assignments, grading, users, and enquiries from a single dashboard. Over £200/month in platform fees was eliminated because the business now owns its own system outright. And the website ranks for 22+ local service areas on Google, creating a steady pipeline of new enquiries that wasn't there before.
