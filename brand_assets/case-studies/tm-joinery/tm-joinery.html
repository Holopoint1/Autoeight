The Problem

TM Joinery Ltd is a construction and joinery services company based near Glasgow, delivering kitchens, extensions, decking, doors, and cabinetry. The business had built a strong reputation for quality craftsmanship, but 100% of new work came through word-of-mouth referrals and occasional social media posts. There was no website, no search visibility, and no way for potential customers searching online to find the business.

The result was an unpredictable lead pipeline entirely dependent on existing networks. The business needed a professional digital presence that would generate inbound enquiries from new customers across their service area — without relying on referrals alone.

What We Did

We designed and built a conversion-focused website with full technical SEO — engineered to rank on Google across multiple regions and convert visitors into qualified quote requests.

Conversion Design

A website built to win trust instantly

Premium design with clear service pages, professional imagery, and a layout optimised for conversion. Every element — from the colour scheme to the page structure — is designed so a potential customer sees quality and credibility within seconds of landing on the site.

Local SEO

Structured to rank across 6 regions

Full technical SEO implementation including structured data, local business schema, service area targeting across 6 regions around Glasgow, and optimised metadata. Google receives a complete, machine-readable profile of the business — services, location, opening hours, and ratings — in the format it rewards with higher rankings.

Automated Leads

Quote requests without manual follow-up

An automated quote request system where customers select their service, describe their project, and submit directly. Enquiries are delivered to the team instantly. Combined with click-to-call, embedded maps, and prominently displayed 5-star Google reviews, the site builds trust and captures leads around the clock.

Social Proof

Completed projects that sell the service

A portfolio section showcasing completed work — kitchens, decking, doors — with real project photography. Visitors see the quality of output before they reach the contact form, reducing the sales cycle and increasing the conversion rate of site visitors to enquiries.

The Result

TM Joinery went from zero online presence to ranking on Google across Glasgow, Eaglesham, East Renfrewshire, Newton Mearns, East Kilbride, and Lanarkshire. The business now has an inbound lead pipeline that operates independently of referrals — quote requests come in through the website automatically, and the 5-star rating and project portfolio build trust before the team even picks up the phone. The company moved from being invisible online to having a professional digital presence that generates qualified leads across its entire service area.
