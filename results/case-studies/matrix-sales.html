The Problem

Matrix TSL makes engineering training equipment for UK universities and colleges. Their sales team was good — but their tools were holding them back. Every rep had a dozen browser tabs open at once: HubSpot for deals, LinkedIn for prospecting, spreadsheets for tracking, Google for researching universities, and notebooks for everything else.

Finding the right person to contact at a university meant hours of manual searching. Matching their products to what a university actually teaches was guesswork. And industry news that could spark a sale? Nobody had time to read it.

What We Did

We built one platform that replaced everything. The team now opens a single tool every morning and has everything they need.

AI Chatbot

Ask it anything about the products

We built an AI assistant that knows every single product Matrix TSL sells. A rep can type "what do we have for renewable energy courses?" and get instant answers with talking points. No more searching through catalogues.

Live Pipeline

See every deal in real time

All their HubSpot deals, stages, and forecasts show up live on the platform. No more switching to HubSpot to check where things stand. It updates automatically and works even when HubSpot is slow.

Contact Finder

Find the right people at any university

Type in a university website and the platform scrapes the staff pages to find engineering lecturers — sorted by discipline (electrical, mechanical, aerospace, etc.) with their emails and phone numbers. Hours of research done in seconds.

News Feeds

Spot opportunities before anyone else

30+ industry news feeds from education, engineering, and renewables sectors — automatically scored by relevance. When a college gets funding or launches a new course, the team sees it that morning.

Email & Reports

Write emails and reports with AI help

Need a follow-up email? The AI drafts it. Need a sales report? It generates one. Need a presentation? There's a tool for that too. All built into the same platform.

Secure

Locked down for the team only

Two layers of login security, encrypted passwords, and a full log of who did what and when. Only the sales team gets in, and everything is tracked.

The Result

The Matrix TSL sales team now starts every day in one place. They check their pipeline, read the latest industry news, ask the AI about products, find contacts at target universities, draft emails, and generate reports — all without switching tools. What used to take a morning of tab-switching and manual research now takes minutes.
