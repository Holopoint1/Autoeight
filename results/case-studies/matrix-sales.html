The Challenge

Matrix TSL is a UK-based manufacturer of engineering education equipment, selling to universities, colleges, and training providers across the country. Their sales team was operating across disconnected tools — HubSpot for deals, LinkedIn for prospecting, spreadsheets for tracking, and manual research for every new lead. There was no unified view of the pipeline, no intelligent way to identify prospects, and no systematic approach to curriculum-aligned selling.

They needed a single platform that brought everything together — CRM data, AI intelligence, prospect research, content creation, and pipeline visibility — tailored specifically to the UK education sector.

What We Built

We built a comprehensive sales intelligence platform from the ground up — 29 pages of tools, dashboards, and AI-powered features, all connected to their existing CRM and tailored to the education market.

AI

AI Sales Assistant & Curriculum Mapper

A Gemini-powered chatbot trained on Matrix TSL's full product catalogue and curriculum mapping data. It recommends products based on university courses, generates sales talking points, and maintains conversation context across sessions.

CRM Integration

Live HubSpot Pipeline

Read-only HubSpot integration with intelligent pagination, retry logic, and stale cache fallback. The team sees live deal data, pipeline stages, owner assignments, and forecasts — all updated in real time with graceful degradation if the API is slow.

Lead Generation

Intelligent Web Scraping

University staff scraper with smart table detection, role classification across 8 engineering disciplines, email validation, and name verification. Plus a LinkedIn profile extractor with batch processing and skill tagging for targeted outreach.

Intelligence

News & Competitor Monitoring

30+ curated RSS feeds from FE/HE education, engineering, and renewables sectors — with a priority scoring algorithm that weights UK relevance, education category, and sales lead potential. Plus competitor intelligence tracking.

Content

Email Crafter & Report Generator

AI-assisted email composition with templates, an automated report generator for custom sales reports, and a presentation creator for slide deck generation — all integrated into the daily sales workflow.

Security

Enterprise Auth & Audit

Two-factor entry (access key + login), bcrypt password hashing, session management with httpOnly cookies, IP-based rate limiting, path traversal protection, and full audit logging of all security events.

Key Features

  • Gemini 2.5 Flash AI integration for chatbot, curriculum mapping, and report generation
  • HubSpot CRM read-only integration with intelligent pagination and stale cache fallback
  • University staff scraper with Cheerio parsing and 8-discipline classification
  • LinkedIn profile extractor with batch processing and role tagging
  • 30+ RSS feed aggregator with priority scoring and deduplication
  • Deal forecasting dashboard with Chart.js visualisations
  • UK regional mapping with Leaflet, postcode API, and OSM routing
  • Canvas-animated circuit board hero backgrounds
  • Academic calendar, syllabus mapping, and product catalogue tools
  • Team notes, to-do lists, and internal collaboration features
  • Helmet.js CSP, rate limiting, and comprehensive audit logging

The Result

Matrix TSL's sales team now operates from a single intelligence platform purpose-built for the UK education market. They identify prospects with AI-powered curriculum matching, track their entire pipeline in real time, generate personalised outreach with AI assistance, and monitor industry news for sales opportunities — all without switching between tools. The platform replaced a scattered collection of spreadsheets, browser tabs, and manual processes with 29 integrated tools that work together.
