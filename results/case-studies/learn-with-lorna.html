The Challenge

Lorna is a qualified GCSE Geography and Maths teacher and AQA Approved Examiner based in Halifax, West Yorkshire. She was running her online tutoring business through a patchwork of tools — a basic Wix website for enquiries, spreadsheets for tracking student progress, email for distributing assignments, and WhatsApp for parent updates.

She needed a single platform where students could access their work, parents could monitor progress, and she could manage everything from one admin dashboard — without paying monthly fees for a bloated LMS she'd never fully use.

What We Built

We built a complete tutoring platform from scratch — a professional marketing site to attract new students, and a full backend system to manage the business.

Marketing Site

Conversion-Focused Landing Pages

A warm, approachable website with typewriter hero animations, 3D card tilt effects, magnetic button interactions, and scroll-reveal animations. Testimonials, a portal preview section, and a dual-channel contact form that sends to both email and the database simultaneously.

Student Portal

Student Dashboard

Students log in to view assigned work, submit responses, track their grades, and see feedback from Lorna. Assignments are filtered by status — pending, submitted, and reviewed — with downloadable file attachments.

Parent Portal

Parent Dashboard

Parents get visibility into their children's progress — subject breakdowns, grade cards, recent activity, and assignment status. Linked accounts let parents with multiple children switch between profiles.

Admin Panel

Tutor Dashboard

Lorna manages everything from one place — create and assign work, upload files with drag-and-drop, review submissions, grade with feedback, manage users, and handle incoming enquiries. Full CRUD on every entity.

Key Features

  • JWT authentication with Google OAuth and email/password login
  • Role-based access — student, parent, and owner roles with database-level filtering
  • Assignment lifecycle: create, assign, submit, review, grade with feedback
  • Drag-and-drop file uploads with download support
  • Dual-channel enquiry form — email via FormSubmit + saved to D1 database
  • Honeypot spam protection on all public forms
  • Typewriter effect, 3D perspective card tilts, and magnetic button hover interactions
  • Geographic SEO targeting 22+ Yorkshire service areas
  • SEND inclusive messaging throughout the site

The Result

Lorna now runs her entire tutoring business from a single custom platform. Students access their work and submit assignments directly. Parents check in on progress without needing to ask. And Lorna manages everything — assignments, grading, enquiries, and users — from one admin dashboard. No monthly LMS fees, no scattered tools, no manual data entry.
