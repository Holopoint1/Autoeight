Halifax Round Table

The Challenge

Halifax Round Table is a young men's social and charitable organisation for members aged 18–45, part of the international Round Table movement founded in 1927. They were managing everything through a Facebook group and WhatsApp — event coordination, member updates, charity plans, news posts. There was no central hub, no way to manage content properly, and no structured way for different committee roles to handle their responsibilities.

They needed a platform that reflected their heritage and values while giving the committee the tools to actually run the organisation digitally.

What We Built

We designed and developed a complete community platform from the ground up — no templates, no WordPress, no compromises.

Frontend

Public-Facing Website

7 custom pages including home, about, history, charity, events gallery, news/blog, and contact — all with scroll-triggered animations, an animated emblem, and a WebGL shader background effect on the contact page.

Dashboard

Member Portal

A full member dashboard with role-based access control across 6 roles — Chairman, Vice Chairman, President, Social Manager, Member, and Honorary Member — each with granular permissions for content, mail, and member management.

Content System

Post & Mail Management

A complete post management system with CRUD operations, categories, draft/published states, image galleries, and read-time calculation. Plus an internal mail system with read/unread tracking, starred messages, and priority levels.

Design

Premium Branding

A bespoke design system built around Round Table's heritage — Playfair Display headings, gold accent palette, and a cohesive visual identity across every page and component. Fully responsive down to mobile.

Key Features

  • Role-based authentication with 16 granular permissions
  • Internal messaging system with categories, priority, and starred messages
  • Blog/news system with category filtering, image galleries, and status management
  • Animated stat counters (300+ branches, 30k+ members, 60+ countries)
  • Intersection Observer scroll animations across all pages
  • WebGL 2.0 fragment shader with procedural noise on the contact page
  • FAQ accordion, embedded video, and Facebook feed integration
  • Events timeline with category filtering
  • Seed data system for demo content and onboarding

The Result

Halifax Round Table now has a professional digital home that matches the prestige of the organisation. Committee members manage content, communicate internally, and coordinate events through a single platform — with the right level of access for each role. The public site showcases their history, charity work, and community spirit with a design that commands attention.
