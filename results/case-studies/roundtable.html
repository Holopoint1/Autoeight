The Problem

Halifax Round Table is part of an international charitable movement that's been going since 1927. They're a group of young men aged 18–45 who organise social events and raise money for local charities. But everything was running through a Facebook group and WhatsApp. Event plans got buried in chat. News posts were lost in the feed. And there was no way for the Chairman, Vice Chairman, or Social Manager to manage things differently based on their role.

They needed a proper digital home — somewhere that told their story publicly and gave the committee real tools to run things behind the scenes.

What We Did

We built them two things: a public website that shows the world who they are, and a private members' area where the committee actually runs the organisation.

Public Site

Their story, told properly

7 custom pages covering their history (going back to 1927), their charity work, upcoming events, news, and how to join. Smooth animations as you scroll, animated stats showing 300+ branches and 30,000+ members worldwide, and a contact page with a visual effect that genuinely makes people stop and look.

Members Area

The right access for the right people

6 different roles — Chairman, Vice Chairman, President, Social Manager, Member, and Honorary Member. Each one sees only what they need and can only do what their role allows. The Chairman manages everything. A regular member can write posts and view the directory. Simple and clear.

Comms

Posts and messages in one place

Members write news posts with photos, save drafts, and publish when ready. They also have an internal mail system — no more relying on WhatsApp for committee decisions. Messages can be starred, categorised, and marked as priority.

Design

A look that matches their heritage

Gold accents, elegant serif headings, and a spinning emblem on the homepage. The design feels premium and historic — exactly what you'd expect from an organisation inspired by the Knights of the Round Table. Works perfectly on phone, tablet, and desktop.

The Result

Halifax Round Table now has a platform that matches the prestige of the organisation. The public sees a beautiful website that tells a nearly 100-year-old story. Behind the scenes, the committee manages posts, messages, events, and members from a single dashboard — each person seeing exactly what their role needs. No more lost WhatsApp messages. No more buried Facebook posts. Just one place for everything.
