The Challenge

TM Joinery Ltd is a two-man joinery business based in Eaglesham, Glasgow, run by Euan and Kieran. They were getting all their work through word-of-mouth and Facebook posts. They had no website, no Google visibility, and no structured way for potential customers to find them, see their work, or request a quote online.

They needed a website that looked as premium as their craftsmanship — and more importantly, one that actually ranked in local search results across Glasgow and the surrounding areas.

What We Built

We designed and developed a conversion-focused website with enterprise-grade SEO — the kind of technical setup that big agencies charge thousands for, built specifically for a local trades business.

Design

Premium Visual Identity

A goldenrod and charcoal colour scheme that communicates quality and professionalism. Playfair Display headings, parallax hero scrolling, staggered scroll animations, and micro-interactions on every interactive element — from ripple buttons to sliding link underlines.

SEO

Full Technical SEO Suite

Three layers of JSON-LD structured data — HomeAndConstructionBusiness schema with opening hours and service areas, individual review markup for rich snippets, and WebSite schema with organisation details. Plus Open Graph, Twitter Cards, geo-coordinates, and an XML sitemap.

Conversion

Lead Generation System

A service-specific contact form with dropdown selection, integrated through FormSubmit for reliable email delivery. Combined with prominent click-to-call buttons, Google Maps embed, and social proof through 6 verified Google reviews displayed directly on the page.

Portfolio

Project Showcase

A Facebook-integrated project carousel that auto-rotates through completed work with manual navigation dots. High-quality project photography displayed in a responsive grid, with lazy loading for performance.

Key Features

  • 3 layers of JSON-LD structured data (LocalBusiness, Reviews, WebSite)
  • Open Graph and Twitter Card meta tags for social sharing
  • Geo-coordinates and ICBM tags for precise local targeting
  • 6 Google reviews embedded directly with star ratings
  • Rotating word animation in hero section (7 service keywords)
  • Facebook post carousel with auto-rotation and manual controls
  • Parallax scrolling, counter animations, and staggered reveals
  • Honeypot spam protection on contact form
  • XML sitemap and robots.txt with AI crawler permissions
  • Fully responsive with 4 breakpoints (1024px, 768px, 480px, sub-480px)

The Result

TM Joinery now has a professional online presence that matches the quality of their work. The site ranks in local search across Glasgow, Eaglesham, East Renfrewshire, Newton Mearns, East Kilbride, and Lanarkshire. Quote requests come in through the website regularly, and their Google reviews and project portfolio build trust before anyone even picks up the phone.
