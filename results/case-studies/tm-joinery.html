The Problem

Euan and Kieran run TM Joinery in Eaglesham, just outside Glasgow. They do kitchens, extensions, decking, doors, cabinetry — proper craftsmanship. But all their work came through word-of-mouth and the occasional Facebook post. If someone in Glasgow googled "joiner near me", TM Joinery didn't exist.

They needed a website. But not just any website — one that would actually show up on Google when people in their area searched for a joiner. And it had to look as good as their work.

What We Did

We built them a website that does two things really well: it looks premium and it ranks on Google.

Design

A site that matches their craftsmanship

Gold and charcoal colour scheme, elegant typography, smooth animations as you scroll, and a hero section that cycles through their services. When a homeowner lands on this site, they instantly see quality — before they've even read a word.

SEO

Set up so Google can actually find them

We told Google exactly what TM Joinery is — a construction business in Eaglesham, open Mon–Fri, serving 6 areas around Glasgow, with a perfect 5-star rating. We added their reviews, their services, their location coordinates, and their opening hours — all in a format Google understands and rewards.

Leads

Quote requests come in on autopilot

A simple form where customers pick the service they need and describe their project. It goes straight to Euan and Kieran's inbox. Plus a click-to-call button, Google Maps showing their area, and all 6 of their 5-star reviews right on the page to build trust.

Portfolio

Their best work, front and centre

A rotating carousel that shows off completed projects — kitchens, decking, doors — pulled straight from their Facebook page. Visitors see real photos of real work before they even scroll to the contact form.

The Result

TM Joinery went from invisible online to ranking on Google across Glasgow, Eaglesham, East Renfrewshire, Newton Mearns, East Kilbride, and Lanarkshire. Quote requests now come in through the website without anyone having to ask for referrals. Their 5-star reviews and project photos do the selling before the phone even rings.
