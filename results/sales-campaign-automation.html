Case Study

Sales campaign automation for a West Yorkshire sales team

CRM to map to inbox. A custom workflow that puts HubSpot data on a live map, plans visit routes and writes personalised emails straight into Outlook drafts. Saves each sales rep around 5 hours a week.

The full walkthrough. Open on YouTube

The challenge

The sales team visits colleges and universities across the UK. To make each trip worthwhile, reps would book one appointment, then try to fill the rest of the trip with nearby customers. That meant:

  • Searching HubSpot for customers in the area
  • Cross-referencing every customer on Google Maps
  • Copying names and email addresses into Outlook one by one
  • Writing the emails for each campaign by hand

Planning a single trip could take hours.

What we built

A single workflow that connects the tools the team already uses.

Live CRM data on a map

Every customer in HubSpot is plotted by address, showing deal value, open opportunities and last activity. Data is read-only, so nothing in the CRM changes.

Route and vicinity planning

Pick a starting appointment, see every customer nearby and plan the route.

Campaign builder

Select the contacts, pick a template made for that type of customer and personalise each email, including a custom signature.

Straight to drafts

Emails are created in Outlook drafts. Nothing sends automatically, so every email is checked before it goes out.

The result

The team estimated the old process took 1 to 2 hours a day. Taking the lower end:

1 hr
saved per rep, per day
5 hrs
saved per rep, per week
260 hrs
saved per rep, per year

Across a whole team, and with more workflows like this, the time adds up fast.
