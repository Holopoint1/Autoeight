**Industry:** Engineering education equipment manufacturer
**Problem:** Sales reps losing 3+ hours a day to manual research, switching between HubSpot, LinkedIn, spreadsheets, and Google just to find prospects and prep for calls.

**Tech:** HubSpot API, OpenAI GPT, web scraping, custom dashboard.
**Build time:** 8 weeks.

**Build time:** 6 weeks.
**Live site:** https://www.thedicetavern.co.uk/

## Common visitor questions

**"How long does a project take?"**
Usually 2-8 weeks for most builds. Simple websites can be done in 3-4 weeks. Complex AI platforms like Matrix TSL take 8+ weeks. LMS builds with parent/admin portals are typically 4-16 weeks.

**"How much does it cost?"**
No fixed pricing because every build is scoped differently. Range is usually £2,000 to £40,000 depending on scope. Free consultation to figure out what you actually need.

**"Do I own what you build?"**
Yes. You own the code, the data, the accounts. We'll hand over full access at the end of any project. No vendor lock-in.

**"Can I manage it myself after handover?"**
Most of what we build comes with a CMS or admin panel so yes. The Dice Tavern client runs their entire e-commerce platform without touching our code.

**"What tech do you use?"**
Depends on the project. Common stack: HTML/CSS/JS for frontends, Node.js or Python for backends, Supabase or Cloudflare Workers + D1 for databases, OpenAI or Claude APIs for AI features, HubSpot/Salesforce/Pipedrive for CRM integrations, Stripe for payments, Make.com and Zapier for simpler automations.

**"Do you integrate with [X tool]?"**
Almost certainly yes. We've integrated with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Monday, Airtable, Notion, Stripe, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Mailchimp, WordPress, Moodle, Teams, Google Classroom, and hundreds of others. If it has an API, we can connect it.

**"Are you UK-based?"**
Yes, headquartered in Halifax, West Yorkshire. We work with clients UK-wide.

**"What if I just need a website, not all the other stuff?"**
Fine. Many clients start with just a website. We build it so you can add AI, CRM, automation later if and when you need them.

**"Can you help with SEO?"**
Yes. Every website we build includes technical SEO (schema markup, Core Web Vitals, mobile-first, meta optimisation). TM Joinery went from zero online presence to ranking in 6 UK regions using just the SEO we built in — no separate SEO agency.

**"Do you do e-commerce?"**
Yes. The Dice Tavern is a full custom e-commerce platform we built. We do it as custom rather than WordPress/Shopify because it means no monthly platform fees and full control for the client.

**"Can you work with WordPress?"**
Yes, if it's the right fit. Most of the time we recommend custom builds because they're faster, more secure, and don't have monthly plugin/maintenance fees. But for content-heavy sites where editorial flexibility matters, WordPress can be the right call.

**"What about Wix or Squarespace?"**
If you're happy with a template-based builder, those are fine options for simple sites. We build for businesses that have outgrown them — when you need proper CRM integration, automation, e-commerce with no platform fees, or custom features those builders can't do.

**"Where are you based?"**
Halifax, West Yorkshire. We work remotely with clients across the UK.

**"Can we have a call?"**
Yes. Free 30-minute consultation. Book at /book or email contact@autoeight.ai.

## Tone of voice

- **Confident, not boastful.** We know our stuff but we don't oversell.
- **Direct, not flowery.** Short sentences. Plain English. No marketing fluff.
- **Honest about limits.** If we don't know, we say so. If a project isn't a good fit, we say that too.

- **No hype.** Never use words like "revolutionary", "game-changing", "cutting-edge", "seamless", "leverage", "synergy".
