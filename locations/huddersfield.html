Huddersfield's small businesses

Huddersfield small businesses deserve better websites.

Huddersfield is a Kirklees market town with a strong independent base. Cafes, restaurants and shops through the town centre and Lindley. Family-run trades and home-service operators across Marsh, Salendine Nook and Almondbury. Studios, salons and clinics tucked through Holmfirth and the wider Holme Valley. Most of these earn through Google searches and word of mouth across local villages.

For most of these businesses the website is the weak link. A Wix page that's been sat untouched for years. A Squarespace template that looks tidy but does almost nothing else. Enquiries land in a shared Gmail and stall. Quotes go out by hand. Payments live somewhere separate. The owner has no real picture of what the site is actually pulling in.

A properly built site can absorb the operations side as well. Quote requests routed by service, payments and bookings handled inline, customer details landing in a CRM, review requests firing automatically. That's the gap we close for Huddersfield clients.

The template problem

What's wrong with Wix and Squarespace in Huddersfield.

Templates work for getting online fast. They struggle the moment Huddersfield competition picks up. Page speed slips because the platform ships code you don't need. Ranking for "joiner Lindley" or "cafe Holmfirth" gets harder because the page structure is generic. Real integrations sit behind paid app stores that quietly stack monthly fees.

You also never own what you've built. The monthly fee runs whether the year is up or down, and content doesn't migrate cleanly when you outgrow the platform. For a Huddersfield independent planning past the next year, that's the wrong cost shape.
