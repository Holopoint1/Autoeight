The template problem

Why rented templates hold Hebden Bridge businesses back.

Template platforms get you online in a weekend. The trouble starts when your business needs anything specific. Want your ceramics shop to take deposits on commissions? That's a paid app. A proper till-to-website stock link? Another app, another monthly fee. Pages load slowly because the platform ships code you'll never use, and ranking for "gift shop Hebden Bridge" is an uphill fight because the page structure was designed for everyone on earth at once.

Then there's ownership. Independents here rightly dislike being beholden to big platforms, yet a subscription site is exactly that. Stop paying and it vanishes. A custom build flips the deal. One project cost, then the site is yours for good.

FAQ

Common questions from Hebden Bridge businesses.

Can we meet in person in Hebden Bridge?

Yes. We're based in Halifax, about eight miles down the valley, so Hebden Bridge is roughly a twenty minute drive along the A646. Kickoff can happen at your shop or studio, or over a coffee near St George's Square. Mid-build reviews and handover work the same way. A remote agency can't do any of that.

Can you add AI and automation, or do you only build the website?

The automation is usually the best bit. We set up order and review-request emails that send themselves, AI-drafted replies to enquiries, bookings and deposits handled online, and CRM sync, so a small Hebden Bridge business gets the back office of a much bigger one.

What can the site actually do beyond looking good?

Take card payments through Stripe, sell your work online, handle bookings and deposits, send order and review-request emails on its own, push every enquiry into a CRM like HubSpot or Pipedrive, draft replies with AI, and show you a dashboard of what's selling. If it involves a form, an order or a customer, we can automate it.

Why not just use Wix, Squarespace or Etsy?

Those platforms rent you a generic site and take a cut or a monthly fee forever. A custom build is yours outright. It loads faster, ranks better for Hebden Bridge searches because the structure is built around your business, and the integrations run properly on the server instead of through bolt-on apps. Etsy is fine as a shop window, but the buyers there are Etsy's customers, not yours.
