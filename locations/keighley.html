Keighley's small businesses

Two valleys, one town, a lot of good businesses.

Keighley sits where the Worth meets the Aire, and its economy runs in both directions. Up the Worth Valley you've got Haworth, Oakworth and Oxenhope, with the steam railway and the Bronte connection pulling visitors through all year. Along the Aire you've got Silsden and Steeton, and Skipton a short run up the road. The town itself keeps its engineering and manufacturing roots alongside the usual mix of trades, garages, salons, shops and cafes.

That geography shapes how customers find you. A guest house in Haworth is competing for visitor searches from all over the country. A heating engineer in Keighley is fighting for a handful of local phrases against firms from Bingley and Bradford. Either way, the site doing the ranking needs to be quick, well structured and written for the actual searches, and most sites round here aren't.

Fixing that is what we do. A hand-built site that loads fast, ranks for your patch and turns the visit into an enquiry, a booking or a sale.

The template problem

Where template platforms let Keighley down.

A Squarespace or Wix site treats a Worth Valley B&B the same as a Californian startup, and it shows. The pages ship heavy code that slows everything on rural mobile signal, the search structure is one-size-fits-all, and anything clever, like a booking calendar or a customer database, means stacking paid apps on top of the base subscription. Competing for "holiday cottage Haworth" or "plumber Keighley" from inside that box is harder than it needs to be.

Then there's the bill that never ends. Years of subscription money and nothing owned at the end of it. We think a Keighley business is better off paying once for a site that belongs to it, the same way it owns its tools, its stock and its premises.

FAQ

Common questions from Keighley businesses.

Are you actually local to Keighley?

We're in Halifax, about half an hour away over the tops. Near enough to drive up for the kickoff meeting, see your premises, and come back during the build when it helps. You get a local-ish builder without being limited to whoever happens to be in Keighley itself.

Can you handle a business that serves the villages, not just the town?

That's most Keighley businesses, so yes. We build separate location pages for the places you actually cover, whether that's Haworth, Oakworth and Oxenhope up the Worth Valley or Silsden and Steeton along the Aire. Google then shows you to searchers in each of them, not just in the town centre.

What do you build besides the website itself?

Booking systems, Stripe payments, CRM sync with HubSpot or Pipedrive, automated confirmation and review request emails, AI-drafted replies to enquiries, sales dashboards, stock tools and customer portals. The site becomes the front end of how the whole business runs, not just a brochure.

Is a custom site really better value than Wix or Squarespace?

Over any serious stretch of time, yes. Template platforms keep charging month after month and you own nothing when you stop paying. A custom build is a one-off project cost for a faster site you own outright, with local SEO structured properly for Keighley searches from the start.
