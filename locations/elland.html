Own it, don't rent it

The problem with drag-and-drop platforms.

Plenty of Elland businesses are on Wix, Squarespace or an old WordPress theme, and the pattern is always the same. The platform fee never stops, the site slows down as apps pile up, and winning searches like "engineering Elland" or "joiner Elland" is an uphill fight because the underlying pages weren't built for your town or your trade. Connecting the site to a CRM, a payment system or a stock list usually means yet another monthly subscription.

We do it the other way round. You pay once for a build, the integrations are written into the site itself, and the code is yours. Good years or quiet years, there's no landlord taking a monthly cut of your website.

FAQ

What Elland businesses want to know.

Are you close enough to Elland to meet properly?

Very. We're in Halifax, roughly ten minutes down the road, so kickoff meetings happen at your unit, shop or yard rather than on a screen. If something comes up mid-build we can call in the same week. Try getting that from an agency in Leeds or Manchester.

We've already got a website. Can you add AI and automation to it?

Often, yes. If the site's sound we can add automated quote follow-ups, AI-drafted replies and review requests around it without starting over. If it's a rented template that can't be extended, we'll say so, and quote for a rebuild with the automation included from day one.

Can the site do more than sit there and look nice?

That's the whole point of building custom. Enquiries can flow into HubSpot or Pipedrive automatically, Stripe can take payments or deposits, follow-up and review emails can send themselves, AI can draft replies for you to approve, and a dashboard can show which pages and searches bring in work. The site becomes part of how the business runs.

What's wrong with the template site we already have?

Maybe nothing, and we'll tell you if so. But most template sites we review load slowly, rank poorly for town-level searches like electrician Elland, and can't connect to anything without extra paid apps. You also never own them. If a rebuild isn't worth it yet, the free call still leaves you with a straight answer.
