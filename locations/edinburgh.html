Edinburgh's indie estate agencies

A premium market that rewards specialists.

Edinburgh is Scotland's highest-value market and one of its tightest. New Town Georgian terraces command serious money and rarely come up. Stockbridge sits at a similar tier with period flats and townhouses. Morningside and Bruntsfield serve the established family belt. Marchmont is dominated by tenement flats and a meaningful student rental presence. Leith and Portobello carry the regen narrative, both shifting rapidly in price as supply catches up with demand.

Indies dominate this market because buyers expect specialist knowledge on Home Reports, missives, conservation listings and the New Town overlay. The chain platforms don't always fit Scottish workflow, and they certainly don't fit indie pricing. Spreadsheets don't cope with the volume around the festival lettings spike either.

The middle option needed building. We built it.

The problem

Why Edinburgh agencies are losing leads.

A New Town vendor emails asking about market timing for a townhouse. A Stockbridge applicant DMs Instagram about a period flat. A landlord with festival short-lets fires off a question to WhatsApp. A Morningside viewing request fills out the website form on a Sunday evening. Each channel sits in its own queue.

In a market where stock is tight and offers move fast, the speed of the chase decides the instruction. The Sunday email gets a Wednesday reply and the vendor has already booked a valuation with someone else.
