Liverpool's indie estate agencies

A mixed market with strong indie presence.

Liverpool property breaks into clear bands. The south Liverpool premium belt runs through Aigburth, Allerton and Mossley Hill where Victorian villas and Edwardian semis command serious money. Woolton sits at the top of that bracket. Crosby and Formby north of the city run the coastal-family belt. Closer in, Wavertree and Toxteth carry the urban regen story. The waterfront apartment market runs separately on a higher-turnover lettings cycle.

Indies hold meaningful share across the suburbs. Chain CRMs are priced for branch networks twice the size of a Liverpool indie office. The default fallback is spreadsheets and a shared inbox, which works until it doesn't.

There needed to be a middle option built for the actual operation. So we built one.

The problem

Why Liverpool agencies are losing leads.

A Crosby vendor emails on Sunday afternoon hoping for a Monday valuation. An Aigburth applicant DMs Instagram about a Victorian semi. A landlord with five properties around Allerton fires off a maintenance question to WhatsApp. A waterfront flat viewing request lands as an overnight form submission. Four channels, four queues, four people sort of watching them.

The follow-up that closes the instruction is the third email, not the first. That's the one nobody has time to send when the inbox is already 200-deep on a Monday morning.
