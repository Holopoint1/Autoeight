The template problem

Why template sites struggle in York.

Templates get you online in a weekend, and in York that's exactly the problem, because everyone else did the same thing. When a dozen tearooms near the Minster sit on near-identical Squarespace themes, nobody stands out and nobody ranks well. The page structure is generic, load speed is whatever the platform decides, and every useful integration hides behind another monthly app fee.

You also never own what you've built. The subscription keeps running whether it's race week or a wet Tuesday in January, and when you outgrow the platform your content doesn't come with you cleanly. For a York business that trades hard in season and plans past next year, renting the shopfront forever is the wrong deal.

FAQ

Common questions from York businesses.

Do you work with businesses in York?

Yes. We're a Halifax studio, about an hour from York by road. Day to day we work remotely, with video calls and a shared project board, and we drive over for kickoff and key meetings when being in the room helps. We won't pretend to be a York agency. Can you build AI and automation into the site, or is it just web design?

Automation is the bigger half of what we do. AI-drafted replies to enquiries, booking confirmations and review requests that send themselves, and a CRM that stays up to date without anyone typing. The site is what your customers see; the automation behind it is what gives you your evenings back.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?

Because you'd be renting, not owning. With a custom build the code is yours, integrations run server-side instead of through a paid app store, pages load fast, and the SEO is set up for York searches specifically. When the business grows, the site grows with it rather than hitting a platform ceiling.
