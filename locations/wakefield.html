The template problem

Why a Wix site struggles in Wakefield.

Template builders get you online, and that's about it. The pages carry code you never asked for, so they load slowly on the phones most of your customers are using. Winning a search like "plumber Ossett" or "accountant Wakefield" needs proper on-page structure, location pages and schema markup, and the builders make all three awkward. Want a booking system or CRM connection? That's another app, another monthly fee, another login.

And the meter never stops running. You pay every month whether trade is good or quiet, and if you ever leave the platform you take nothing with you. For a Wakefield firm planning to be around in ten years, buying a site you own beats renting one you don't.

FAQ

Common questions from Wakefield businesses.

Do you cover Wakefield?

Yes. We're based in Halifax, about forty minutes from Wakefield down the M62. Close enough that kickoff can happen at your premises or over a coffee in the city centre, and close enough to come back for the mid-build review too. Remote works fine if you'd rather keep it on Zoom.

Can AI and automation be built into the site rather than bolted on later?

That's exactly how we work. AI-drafted enquiry replies, booking confirmations, review requests and CRM updates are wired in during the build, so the site is already doing the admin on the day it goes live. Nothing extra to buy, nothing new to learn.

What can you wire into a Wakefield site beyond the pages?

CRM sync with HubSpot or Pipedrive, Stripe payments, sales dashboards, booking systems, automated confirmation and review request emails, AI-drafted enquiry replies, customer portals and stock tools. If it touches a form, a payment or a customer record, we can connect it.

Why not just use Wix or Squarespace?

Because you'd be renting, not owning. A template platform charges every month forever, loads slower than it should, and makes ranking for Wakefield searches harder because the page structure is generic. We build you a site you own outright, with the integrations running properly server-side.
