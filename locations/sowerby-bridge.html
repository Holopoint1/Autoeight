Beyond the template

Rented templates weren't made for valley towns.

A Squarespace template doesn't know that "restaurant Sowerby Bridge" and "restaurant Halifax" are fought over by different businesses, or that a trade here needs to show up across half a dozen villages along the valley. It gives every business the same generic structure, charges monthly for the privilege, and slows the pages down with code nobody asked for. Bookings, ordering and payments all mean extra apps and extra fees.

Our builds start from your town and your customers. Pages structured for the searches that matter here, hosting that loads fast on a patchy phone signal, and every integration written into the site rather than rented around it. When it's live, it's yours for good.

FAQ

Things Sowerby Bridge businesses ask first.

Will we ever actually see you in Sowerby Bridge?

Often, if you want to. Halifax is ten minutes along the valley, so kickoff happens at your place or somewhere on Wharf Street, and we're happy to call back in as the build progresses. A distant agency does everything by email and video call. We don't have to, and it makes for a better site.

Can AI and automation be part of the build, not an afterthought?

That's how we work by default. We plan the automation alongside the site itself, so from launch day reviews get requested automatically, replies get drafted by AI for you to approve, and bookings confirm themselves. Adding it later works too, but building it in from the start is cheaper and cleaner.

What extras can be built into the website?

Whatever removes admin from your week. Table or appointment bookings, deposits and payments through Stripe, enquiries landing in a CRM such as HubSpot or Pipedrive instead of an inbox, emails that confirm, follow up and ask for reviews on their own, AI-drafted replies you approve before sending, and reporting that shows where customers found you.

Is a custom site really better than a Wix one?

For a business that depends on being found, yes. A custom site is faster, structured to rank for Sowerby Bridge searches, and able to talk to your booking, payment and customer systems directly. And it's a one-off cost you own, rather than rent that never ends. If Wix genuinely covers what you need, we'll say so on the call.
