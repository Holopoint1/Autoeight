Rotherham's small businesses

Rotherham businesses deserve better websites.

Rotherham's business story is broader than people assume. The steel heritage is right there in Magna, sat inside the old Templeborough works, but the modern version of that industry lives on the Advanced Manufacturing Park on the Sheffield boundary, where precision engineering firms and their suppliers cluster. Parkgate pulls steady retail trade from across the borough. And in Wickersley, Wath, Maltby and the town centre there's the usual honest mix, trades with full order books, salons and clinics with loyal regulars, food businesses, garages, fitters and cleaners.

The odd thing is how rarely the website matches the standard of the work. Firms that machine parts to fine tolerances are represented online by a page that hasn't changed since it went up. Trades with month-long waiting lists lose enquiries to a contact form nobody checks until evening. Money arrives through one system, customer details live in another, and nothing joins up, so nobody can say which marketing actually pays.

We treat the website as working kit, not a brochure. It should capture every enquiry, reply fast, take payment, file the customer in a CRM and ask for the review afterwards. That's the standard we build to for Rotherham clients.

The template problem

The trouble with rented templates in Rotherham.

Template platforms sell speed, and to be fair they deliver it. What they don't deliver is an edge. When someone searches "roofer Wickersley" or "landscaper Wath", Google is comparing your page against every other local firm's, and a template gives it nothing distinctive to rank. Load times suffer under code you can't strip out. Bookings, email automation and CRM connections each mean another plugin and another line on the bill.

Worst of all, years of subscription payments leave you owning nothing. Cancel and it's gone. We think a Rotherham business should put that money into an asset instead, a site it owns outright that gets more valuable as it collects content, reviews and rankings over time.
