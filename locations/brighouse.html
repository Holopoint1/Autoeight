The template trap

Why a rented template holds Brighouse businesses back.

Wix and Squarespace look cheap until you add up years of monthly fees for a site you'll never own. The deeper problem is what they can't do. Ranking for "plumber Brighouse" or "cake shop Brighouse" takes proper page structure, fast loading and local signals set up correctly, and template platforms make all three harder. Anything clever, like online ordering or a booking calendar, becomes another paid app bolted on top.

A custom build flips that. One fixed project cost, a site registered against your business and your town, and every integration built in rather than rented. If you stop paying a platform, your site vanishes. If you stop paying us, nothing happens, because it's yours.

FAQ

Questions Brighouse businesses ask us.

Can we meet in person in Brighouse?

Yes. We're based in Halifax, about fifteen minutes away, so a kickoff at your premises or over a coffee near the canal basin is easy to sort. Mid-build reviews and handover can happen face to face as well, which isn't something a Leeds or Manchester agency will offer a Brighouse client.

Could you add AI and automation to what we already do?

Yes. We can fit automation onto an existing site or build it into a new one: replies drafted by AI for you to approve, review requests that send themselves once a job's done, and enquiries dropping straight into a CRM. It's usually the part of a project that saves the most hours.
