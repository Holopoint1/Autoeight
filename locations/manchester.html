The template problem

Wix and Squarespace can't keep up here.

A template site is a fair trade in a quiet market. Manchester isn't one. Platform builders ship heavy code that drags your load times, and Google notices. The page structure can't be shaped around the specific searches your customers run, so you end up with one generic homepage trying to rank against thousands of competitors. Want a booking system, CRM connection or proper analytics? That's an app store, and each app is another monthly charge stacked on top of the subscription.

There's an ownership problem too. Years of paying the platform and you still hold nothing. Try to leave and the content, structure and SEO history don't transfer cleanly. In a market this competitive, handing your only sales asset to a rental platform is a decision worth rethinking.

FAQ

Do you handle AI and automation as well as the website?

Yes, and for most clients it's where the real gains sit. We wire in AI that drafts enquiry replies, follow-up emails that fire on their own, lead scoring and CRM records that update without any typing. You end up with a site that works your pipeline for you, not just a set of pages.
