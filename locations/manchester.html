Manchester's indie estate agencies

A market the size of two cities.

Greater Manchester is genuinely several property markets pretending to be one. Premium family belt sits down through Didsbury, Chorlton and across to Altrincham. The apartment volume game is in Salford Quays, NOMA and the central core. Stockport, Bury and Bolton run their own mid-market suburb stock. The Northern Quarter and Ancoats are flat lettings on a fast turnover cycle.

Indies hold a lot of this market despite the chains. The price gap on premium suburbs goes higher than the chain-focused platforms can comfortably handle, which leaves indie offices running either oversized software they don't use, or spreadsheets and a Gmail thread that's 800 deep.

There needs to be a middle option that's actually built for this size of operation. So we built one.

The problem

Why Manchester agencies are losing leads.

An applicant in Chorlton DMs your Instagram about a flat. A vendor in Altrincham emails the office. A landlord with three units in Salford fires off a maintenance request to WhatsApp at 10pm. A tourist-turned-investor in town wants a viewing on a Sunday. None of these channels know about each other.

By Monday morning the negotiators are catching up on email, the second-follow-up to Friday's hot vendor never sends, and the Altrincham listing goes to the agency that did follow up. It's the volume that hurts you. Not bad sales, just lost leads.
