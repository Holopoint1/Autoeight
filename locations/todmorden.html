Todmorden's small businesses

A proper market town, with websites that lag behind it.

Todmorden punches well above its size. The indoor market and the outdoor stalls beside it keep the town centre trading the way market towns are supposed to. Incredible Edible started here, planting food in public beds and putting the town on the map for visitors who'd otherwise have driven straight past. The Rochdale Canal threads through the middle, Stoodley Pike stands over the town, and the old Lancashire boundary runs right underneath the Town Hall. Half your customers think in Yorkshire terms, the rest come over from Littleborough and Rochdale.

The centre is full of butchers, grocers, cafes, salons, secondhand shops and long-standing stallholders, plus trades and small workshops out in Walsden and Cornholme. What most share is a web presence that undersells them. A Facebook page instead of a site, a template store nobody has updated since launch, a phone number and hope.

Customers on both sides of the border search online first and buy from whoever makes it easiest. A site with payments, bookings and follow-up built in turns that traffic into trade instead of letting it drift to the next town along.

The template problem

Where Wix and Squarespace fall down in Todmorden.

A template gets a page live quickly, then the limits show. Winning searches like "butcher Todmorden" or "cafe Todmorden" is harder on a platform whose page structure is identical for millions of sites. Speed suffers under features you never switched on, and everything genuinely useful, from deposits to stock control to proper booking, is a paid add-on with its own monthly charge.

The deeper issue is the rent. Subscription platforms cost you every month, good year or bad, and the moment you stop paying the site is gone. Monthly rent on a template that never quite fits is a poor place for a careful business to spend money. We charge once, then the site belongs to you.

An online shop that runs alongside the counter. Stripe checkout, click and collect for regulars, delivery further afield, stock updated from your phone, and order confirmation emails sent automatically. The Dice Tavern, our e-commerce build, works on the same bones.

Food & drink

Cafes, bakeries, producers

Menus you edit yourself, table bookings, weekend pre-orders, and a mailing list that brings people back midweek. In a town Incredible Edible made famous for food, ranking for "lunch Todmorden" and "farm shop near Todmorden" is worth real money.

Quote forms straight to your phone, a job gallery that does the selling, service pages for Todmorden, Walsden, Cornholme and over the border, plus automatic review requests when a job finishes. Our TM Joinery site follows this exact pattern.
