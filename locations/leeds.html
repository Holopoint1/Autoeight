Leeds' small businesses

Leeds small businesses deserve better websites.

Leeds is Yorkshire's busiest small-business city. Independent gyms and clinics around Chapel Allerton. Restaurants, bars and creative studios across the city centre. Family trades and home services running out of Horsforth and Kirkstall. Coffee shops, salons and boutiques sat right across Headingley and Roundhay. A lot of money moves through these patches every week, and most of it starts with a Google search.

A lot of Leeds businesses still rely on a Wix or Squarespace site that does barely anything. It looks alright on a phone, and that's about where the value stops. Enquiries get missed in a shared inbox, follow-ups are an afterthought, payments live on a separate platform, and the owner never sees a single useful number about how the site is performing.

A properly built site can do far more than that. Quote requests routed by service, payments and bookings handled on the spot, customer details landing in a CRM, review requests going out automatically. That's the gap we close for Leeds clients.

The template problem

What's wrong with Wix and Squarespace in Leeds.

Templates are fine for a soft launch. They start to creak the moment Leeds competition heats up. Page speed sags because the platform ships code you'll never use. Ranking for "personal trainer Headingley" or "barber Chapel Allerton" gets harder because the on-page structure is generic. Integrations sit behind paid app stores that quietly stack monthly costs.

You also never own what you've built. The monthly fee runs whether the business is booming or slow, and a year of redesign work doesn't migrate cleanly when you outgrow the platform. For a Leeds business that wants to keep growing past the next twelve months, that's the wrong shape of cost.
