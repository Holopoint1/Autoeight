The template problem

What's wrong with Wix and Squarespace in Leeds.

Templates are fine for a soft launch. They start to creak the moment Leeds competition heats up. Page speed sags because the platform ships code you'll never use. Ranking for "personal trainer Headingley" or "barber Chapel Allerton" gets harder because the on-page structure is generic. Integrations sit behind paid app stores that quietly stack monthly costs.

You also never own what you've built. The monthly fee runs whether the business is booming or slow, and a year of redesign work doesn't migrate cleanly when you outgrow the platform. For a Leeds business that wants to keep growing past the next twelve months, that's the wrong shape of cost.

FAQ

Common questions from Leeds businesses.

Could you add AI and automation to our site, or just design it?

Both, and the automation is usually where the value is. We wire in AI-drafted enquiry replies, automatic follow-ups, review requests and CRM sync, so the site does the chasing that normally eats your week. Design gets you found; the automation turns visits into booked work.

What integrations can you build into our site?

Sales dashboards, CRM sync with HubSpot or Pipedrive, Stripe payments, automated order confirmation and review request emails, AI-drafted enquiry replies, booking systems, stock tools, customer portals, lead scoring, and live chat. Anything that touches a form, an order, or a customer can be wired up.

How is this different from Wix or Squarespace?

Templates can only take you so far. You own the custom Leeds site we build, you don't pay forever to rent a template. Integrations run server-side, pages load fast, local SEO is set up properly, and the admin area is built for your workflow rather than a generic dashboard.
