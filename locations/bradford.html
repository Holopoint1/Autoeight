Bradford's small businesses

Bradford small businesses deserve better websites.

Bradford is one of the most diverse small-business cities in the country. Independent restaurants, takeaways and grocers spread across the city centre and Manningham. Family-run shops and trades through Saltaire and Shipley. Salons, gyms and clinics tucked across Eccleshill and Allerton. Studios and creative businesses around the wider Aire Valley. Most of these earn through Google searches and word of mouth.

For most of these businesses the website is the weakest part of the operation. A Wix page set up a few years back. A Squarespace site nobody has touched in months. It looks fine on a phone, and that's about where the value stops. Enquiries land in a shared inbox and slip. Quotes go out manually. Payments live somewhere separate. The owner has no real reporting on what's actually working.

A properly built site can carry the operations side as well. Quote requests routed by service, payments and bookings handled inline, customer details landing in a CRM, review requests firing automatically. That's the gap we close for Bradford clients.

The template problem

What's wrong with Wix and Squarespace in Bradford.

Templates are fine for getting online quickly. They start to creak once Bradford competition picks up. Page speed slips because the platform ships code you'll never use. Ranking for "joiner Saltaire" or "takeaway Shipley" gets harder because the page structure is generic. Real integrations sit behind paid app stores that quietly stack monthly fees.

You also never own what you've built. The monthly fee runs whether the year is up or down, and content doesn't migrate cleanly when you outgrow the platform. For a Bradford independent planning past the next year, that's the wrong cost shape.
