Nottingham's indie estate agencies

A Midlands market split across student and suburb.

Nottingham splits cleanly into two operating modes. The premium leafy belt runs through West Bridgford, The Park, Mapperley Park and parts of Sherwood with detached and semi-detached stock that moves on family sales. Mid-market suburbs like Carlton and Arnold sit behind. Beeston, Lenton and Dunkirk run the student lettings game on a six-month cycle that fills the central inbox.

Indies share the market with the chains here, especially in the leafier postcodes. The chain-focused CRMs aren't built for the cost base of a five-person West Bridgford office, and spreadsheets break around the September student turnover.

The middle option was missing. So we built one.

The problem

Why Nottingham agencies are losing leads.

A West Bridgford vendor emails Sunday wanting a Monday valuation. A student in Beeston DMs Instagram about a four-bed for September. A landlord with seven Lenton units WhatsApps a maintenance question. A Mapperley viewing request fills out the website form overnight. Each channel has its own queue and nobody coordinates.

The hot West Bridgford vendor gets a Wednesday reply because Tuesday morning was eaten by student maintenance tickets. The instruction goes elsewhere. Volume problem, not a sales problem.
