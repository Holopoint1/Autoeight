Halifax's small businesses

Halifax small businesses deserve better websites.

Halifax is a town of independents. Cafes and bakeries clustered around the Piece Hall and the town centre. Joiners, plumbers and electricians working out of Sowerby Bridge and Brighouse. Salons and dog groomers in Northowram and King Cross. Shops and studios spread from Hebden Bridge all the way back through Calderdale. Most of these businesses earn through word of mouth and Google, in roughly that order.

The website is often the weakest part of the operation. A Wix page stitched together a few years ago, a Squarespace site nobody has touched since launch, or a Facebook page doing the job a real site should. It looks fine, but it does almost nothing. Enquiries get missed, follow-ups never happen, payments are clunky, and the owner never sees the data they need to actually grow.

A modern site can carry a lot more weight than that. Quote requests routed to the right person, payments taken on the spot, customer details landing in a CRM, review requests going out automatically. That's the gap we close.

The template problem

What's wrong with Wix and Squarespace in Halifax.

Templates are fine for getting online quickly. They fall short the moment your business does anything specific. Page speed is mediocre because the platform loads code you'll never use. Local SEO for "joiner Halifax" or "cafe Sowerby Bridge" is harder to win because the on-page structure is built for everyone, not for you. Real integrations are bolt-on apps that cost extra each month.

You also never own the site. The monthly fee keeps going whether the business is having a good year or a bad one, and if you cancel you walk away with nothing. For a Calderdale independent that wants to grow rather than tread water, that's the wrong shape of cost.

What we'd build for Halifax industries

Built around what your business actually does.

Different Halifax industries need different sites. A joiner working out of Sowerby Bridge doesn't need what a Piece Hall cafe needs. Here's what a build looks like across the four Halifax business types we see most often.

Trades & contractors

Joiners, plumbers, electricians

Quote request forms wired straight to your phone, Google Business Profile properly configured, service-area SEO across Halifax, Brighouse, Sowerby Bridge and Elland, before-and-after gallery, five-star review capture. Same shape as the site we built for TM Joinery, a Yorkshire joinery business.

Cafes & hospitality

Piece Hall independents, town-centre bakeries

Menu that's easy to update from your phone, online ordering with Stripe, table booking system, loyalty and repeat-customer emails, gallery of the space. Local SEO for "cafe Halifax", "brunch Piece Hall", "coffee Sowerby Bridge".

Salons & personal care

Full booking system with staff calendars, SMS reminders, deposit taking, client history and notes, pre-visit intake forms, review-request automation. So no-shows drop and rebook rate goes up.

Independent retail

Shops, studios, makers

Custom e-commerce with Stripe payments, product photography that loads fast, category and search built for browsing, automated order emails, backend that lets you edit anything yourself. The Dice Tavern build in miniature, adapted to your product range.
