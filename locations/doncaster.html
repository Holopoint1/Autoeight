Doncaster's small businesses

Doncaster businesses deserve better websites.

Doncaster has more going on than it gets credit for. The racecourse pulls big crowds every St Leger week and feeds hospitality across the city all season. iPort by the M18 has turned the area into one of the country's serious logistics hubs, and a whole layer of haulage, warehousing and supply firms has grown up around it. Lakeside has its own pull of retail and leisure trade. And spread through Bentley, Armthorpe and the surrounding villages there's a deep bench of trades, builders, roofers, sparkies, plasterers and gardeners with more work than time.

What most of these businesses share is a website that lags well behind the business itself. A template someone set up years ago that hasn't earned a single enquiry since. Meanwhile customers are searching, comparing three firms in five minutes, and choosing whichever one made it easiest to get in touch. Every enquiry that doesn't get a quick reply is a job handed to a competitor, and without any tracking, the owner never even knows it happened.

We build sites that pull their weight on the operations side too. Enquiries sorted and answered fast, bookings and payments taken on the page, every customer recorded in a CRM, reviews chased automatically. That's what a Doncaster business should expect from its website.

The template problem

Where template sites fall short in Doncaster.

Wix and Squarespace are quick to set up and that's genuinely their best feature. The problems show up later. The platform loads slowly on a phone, which is where most of your customers are. Competing for searches like "electrician Armthorpe" or "roofer Bentley" is hard when the page structure underneath is identical to every other template site Google crawls. And each useful add-on, bookings, email flows, a proper CRM connection, comes from an app store with its own monthly price tag.

The bigger issue is that you're renting, not owning. Stop paying and the site vanishes. Try to move and your content doesn't travel cleanly. A Doncaster business planning for the long run deserves an asset it owns outright, not a subscription with a logo on it.
