The template problem

Why Wix and Squarespace hold Barnsley firms back.

A template got a lot of Barnsley businesses online, and for a while that was enough. It stops being enough once you want the site to actually win work. Pages load slowly because the platform ships a pile of code you never asked for. Trying to rank for "plumber Wombwell" or "joiner Dodworth" is an uphill fight when every page shares the same generic structure as thousands of other sites. And the useful features, proper booking, proper CRM, proper email flows, sit behind app stores that add another monthly charge every time.

Then there's ownership. That subscription keeps going out whether January is dead or the diary is full, and if you ever want to leave, your content doesn't come with you cleanly. For a Barnsley business thinking further ahead than next quarter, renting your own website is the wrong deal.
