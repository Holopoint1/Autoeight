Dewsbury's small businesses

The Heavy Woollen District still works for a living.

Dewsbury has always been a working town. The mills that gave the Heavy Woollen District its name have mostly gone quiet, but the graft hasn't. The town centre still trades around the market, and the streets between Dewsbury, Batley, Heckmondwike and Mirfield are full of joiners, tilers, mechanics, takeaways, fabricators and family firms that have been passed down two or three generations.

What's changed is where the customer starts. Twenty years ago it was a card in a shop window or a mate's recommendation at the club. Now it's a phone search at nine o'clock at night, and the firm with the fast, convincing website gets the enquiry before you've even heard about the job. Too many solid Dewsbury businesses are invisible online while newer rivals with thinner track records take the work.

We build the kind of site that fixes that. One that turns up when your towns search for your trade, answers the enquiry straight away, and keeps every lead where you can see it.

The template problem

A rented template won't cut it here.

Plenty of local firms have a Wix or Squarespace page someone's nephew set up years back. It exists, and that's the kindest thing you can say about it. The platform drags page speed down, the search structure is the same as a florist in Florida, and every useful feature costs another bolt-on app with its own subscription. Ranking for "roofer Dewsbury" or "MOT Batley" against a properly built competitor site is an uphill fight you didn't need to pick.

There's also the ownership question. Rent a template for a decade and you've spent real money on something you can never sell, move or keep. We build sites our clients own outright. Pay for it once, and it's yours the same way your van is.

FAQ

Common questions from Dewsbury businesses.

Do you work with Dewsbury businesses?

Yes, plenty of our patch sits this side of the M62. Halifax to Dewsbury is around twenty five minutes, so we'll happily come to your unit, your shop or your office for the first meeting and again during the build. If remote suits you better, that works too.

Can one site cover Dewsbury, Batley and Heckmondwike together?

Yes, and for most businesses round here it should. Customers in the Heavy Woollen towns cross between them constantly, so we build a location page for each town you serve and structure the site so Google understands your full patch, not just your postcode.

We're a trade business, not a tech company. Is this overkill?

No. The point of the automation is that you never think about it. A quote request pings your phone, the customer gets an instant reply, the job lands in a simple pipeline, and a review request goes out when the work is done. You just answer your phone like you always have.

What's wrong with staying on Wix or Squarespace?

The rent, mostly. Those platforms charge monthly forever and you never own anything at the end of it. On top of that the pages are slower than a hand-built site, and the generic structure makes it tougher to rank for searches like joiner Dewsbury or plumber Batley. A custom site is yours for good.
