The Problem

Hours wasted on admin. Leads slipping through.

Negotiators are burning half the day on admin. Logging enquiries from web forms, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp and SMS. Re-typing the same details into the CRM. Manually chasing follow-ups. Booking viewings by phone tag. Hours of repetition every single day that should be automated.

And because nothing's joined up, leads slip through. The 2nd or 3rd follow-up (the one that wins the instruction) rarely happens. Vendor enquiries get buried under newsletters. Viewing requests sit unanswered for a day too long and the applicant moves on.

The big chain-focused platforms exist but they're priced and built for corporate operations, not independents. The alternative everyone defaults to is spreadsheets, a shared inbox, and a "we'll get to it" pile that grows every week. There was no middle option that automated the admin and used AI to keep on top of every lead. So we built one.

What you get

Six pillars. One platform. Your branding.

Public site

Branded site per agency

Classic property portal layout with tabbed search, full sales and lettings listings, property detail pages with image galleries and JSON-LD, valuation and viewing request forms. Sitemap, robots, OG tags. Everything an estate agency website needs out of the box.

Unified inbox

Every channel in one place

Web forms, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, SMS, live chat. 14 inbound sources surfaced in a single inbox with source bars, urgency flags, filter chips, inline reply, and CSV export. Sidebar badge shows unread across all channels at a glance.

AI classification

AI tags every inbound

AI classifies every email as it arrives: vendor, applicant, landlord, viewing request, or maintenance. It scores urgency, matches the enquiry to a live property where possible, and drafts a reply ready for the negotiator to approve and send.

Chase automation

Follow-up that still feels personal

Automated chase sequences fire on schedule but send from the agent's own mailbox, so deliverability and tone stay personal. Sequences stop the moment the lead replies, books, or is marked dead. No more leads going cold on day three.

Viewings + calendar

Bookings tied to the diary

Viewings booked through the site land in the negotiator's calendar with two-way Google and Outlook sync. Today, Upcoming, Past, Cancelled tabs in admin. One-click confirm, send reminder, cancel, or reschedule. Correspondence timeline on every viewing.

Full admin

One platform, every enquiry

Every enquiry, viewing, vendor conversation, applicant record and team setting sits in one place. Non-technical staff manage everything end to end. No developer involvement, no toggling between tools.

FAQ

The questions every agency asks.

Is this a CRM?

No. This is an automation and enquiry-handling system that sits on top of (or replaces) your existing CRM. Every channel feeds into one inbox, AI classifies and drafts replies, chase sequences fire automatically, and viewings sync to your calendar. It does the work your CRM never has.

How much does it cost?

Custom builds typically start around £2,000 for a focused setup, scaling depending on integrations, the number of branches, and how much existing data needs migrating. Fixed quote per project, no surprise hourly billing. You get a rough budget at the end of the free 30-min audit.

Can I see it before I commit?

Yes. Northbrick.org is the live public site, so you can browse the listings, search, valuation forms and viewing request flow exactly as a vendor or applicant would see them on your branded version. The staff inbox, AI classifier and admin areas are behind login and not open to public access. Book a 15-min walkthrough and we'll demo every screen live.

How long to launch?

3 to 12 weeks from kickoff to your branded version going live, depending on scope. Most agencies are running their first AI-classified enquiry in the platform within 4 weeks of starting.

Who owns the code and data?

You do. Your branded version is yours. The code, the database, the customer relationships. No vendor lock-in. No monthly platform fees if you self-host. We can host it for you if you'd rather not deal with that, but it's a choice not a requirement.

What if I already use an existing estate agency CRM?

You don't have to switch. We can integrate with whatever you're using so the unified inbox, AI classification, and chase sequences sit on top of your existing CRM. Your listings, applicant records, and pipeline stay where they are. Or if you'd rather fully migrate, we move everything across as part of the build. Your call, not ours.
