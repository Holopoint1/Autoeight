The Problem

You're losing leads in your own inbox.

Enquiries arrive across web forms, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp, SMS, and sometimes a sticky note on the negotiator's desk. Nobody triages them properly. Newsletters bury them. The second or third follow-up, the one that actually wins the instruction, rarely happens.

Reapit and Alto exist, but they're priced and built for the chains. The alternative everyone defaults to is spreadsheets, a shared inbox, and a "we'll get to it" pile that grows every week.

There was no middle option that treated enquiries as a unified dataset and used AI to keep on top of them. So we built one.

What you get

Six pillars. One platform. Your branding.

Public site

Branded site per agency

Rightmove-style homepage with tabbed search, full sales and lettings listings, property detail pages with image galleries and JSON-LD, valuation and viewing request forms. Sitemap, robots, OG tags. Everything an estate agency website needs out of the box.

Unified inbox

Every channel in one place

Web forms, Gmail, Outlook, WhatsApp, Instagram, Messenger, SMS, live chat. 14 inbound sources surfaced in a single inbox with source bars, urgency flags, filter chips, inline reply, and CSV export. Sidebar badge shows unread across all channels at a glance.

AI classification

Claude tags every inbound

Claude Haiku classifies every email as it arrives: vendor, applicant, landlord, viewing request, or maintenance. It scores urgency, matches the enquiry to a live property where possible, and drafts a reply ready for the negotiator to approve and send.

Chase automation

Follow-up that still feels personal

Automated chase sequences fire on schedule but send from the agent's own mailbox, so deliverability and tone stay personal. Sequences stop the moment the lead replies, books, or is marked dead. No more leads going cold on day three.

Viewings + calendar

Bookings tied to the diary

Viewings booked through the site land in the negotiator's calendar with two-way Google and Outlook sync. Today, Upcoming, Past, Cancelled tabs in admin. One-click confirm, send reminder, cancel, or reschedule. Correspondence timeline on every viewing.

Full admin

Nine modules, one platform

Dashboard with KPIs, property and photo management, full site CMS, unified inbox, viewings, live chat, response-time stats, SEO analytics, and team settings. Non-technical staff manage everything. No developer involvement required.

FAQ

The questions every agency asks.

Is this Reapit?

No. Reapit and Alto are built and priced for chains. This is a custom-built alternative for independent agencies. Same depth of features. Priced to fit a one to ten branch operation.

How much does it cost?

Custom builds typically start around £4,000 for a focused setup, scaling depending on integrations, the number of branches, and how much existing data needs migrating. Fixed quote per project, no surprise hourly billing. You get a rough budget at the end of the free 30-min audit.

Can I see it before I commit?

Yes. Northbrick.org is the live public site, so you can browse the listings, search, valuation forms and viewing request flow exactly as a vendor or applicant would see them on your branded version. The staff inbox, AI classifier and admin areas are behind login and not open to public access. Book a 15-min walkthrough and we'll demo every screen live.

How long to launch?

6 to 12 weeks from kickoff to your branded version going live, depending on scope. Most agencies are running their first AI-classified enquiry in the platform within 4 weeks of starting.

Who owns the code and data?

You do. Your branded version is yours. The code, the database, the customer relationships. No vendor lock-in. No monthly platform fees if you self-host. We can host it for you if you'd rather not deal with that, but it's a choice not a requirement.

What if I already use Reapit / Alto / Jupix?

Migration is part of the build. We move your existing listings, applicant database, and historical correspondence across. We can also run the platform alongside your existing CRM during transition if you'd rather not switch overnight.
