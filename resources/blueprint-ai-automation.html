How It Works

From idea to live in two weeks

We don't rip out your existing tools. We build automation around them.

1

Discovery Audit

We map every manual process eating your team's time. Usually in a single 45-minute session. We look at inboxes, spreadsheets, CRMs, and communication tools to identify exactly where automation will have the biggest impact.

2

Build & Integrate

We build the automation workflows and connect them to your existing tools. No new software to learn. Everything pipes into the systems you already use (CRM, email, Slack, Google Sheets, etc.).

3

Test & Refine

We run the automations in parallel with your current process so you can verify they work exactly as expected before cutting over. Typically takes 3–5 days.

4

Launch & We Monitor

We switch on, watch the dashboards, and handle any edge cases. You get a live monitoring view so you can see every automation running in real time. We maintain everything ongoing. Nothing breaks quietly.
