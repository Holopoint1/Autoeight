How It Works

Your automated sales engine, live in two weeks

We build the entire outreach and follow-up system around your existing CRM and email tools.

1

Pipeline Audit

We map your current sales workflow from lead to close — identifying every manual touchpoint where a rep's time is being spent on something a machine could do. Usually 8–12 steps can be automated immediately.

2

Automation Build

We build the lead research, personalised outreach, and multi-step follow-up sequences. AI researches each prospect and generates personalised opening lines — so your emails feel human even at scale.

3

CRM Integration

Every interaction is logged automatically. Replies, opens, clicks, and bookings all flow straight into your CRM — so your pipeline data is always accurate without your reps touching it.

4

Launch

Your team focuses entirely on conversations and closes. We monitor the automation dashboards, handle edge cases, and refine messaging based on reply rates. Typically improves by 20–40% in the first month.
