How It Works

Your email engine, built in two weeks

We design, write, and automate your entire email system, from welcome to win-back.

1

Sequence Mapping

We map your customer journey, from first touch to sale and beyond. We identify every email touchpoint: welcome, nurture, follow-up, re-engagement, upsell. Most businesses have 3–8 sequences to build.

2

Copy & Build

We write the emails (or work with your existing copy) and build the sequences in your email platform. We handle timing, personalisation tokens, and conditional branching, so the right message reaches the right person at the right time.

3

Trigger Integration

Sequences connect to your lead sources. Web forms, CRM, landing pages, event registrations. When someone enters your system, the right sequence fires automatically. No manual enrolment needed.

4

Launch & Optimise

We monitor open rates, click rates, and reply rates in the first 2 weeks and refine subject lines, timing, and content. Ongoing optimisation keeps performance compounding month over month.
