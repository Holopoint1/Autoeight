What is sales automation?

Sales automation uses AI and workflow tools to handle the repetitive parts of your sales process automatically. This includes finding and researching prospects, writing personalised outreach emails, sending multi-step follow-up sequences, booking meetings, and keeping your CRM updated — all without your sales team doing it manually. AI adds a layer of intelligence by researching each prospect and personalising every message based on real data.

Sales automation is built for B2B companies with active outbound sales teams, businesses scaling their pipeline without adding headcount, and any company where sales reps spend more time on admin than on actual selling.

How It Works

How we build your sales pipeline

From finding prospects to booking meetings, every part of the outreach process is handled by automation so your team can focus on real conversations and closing.

Step 1

Prospect Discovery

We set up systems that find and qualify leads that match your ideal customer. Fresh prospects land in your pipeline every day without anyone spending hours on manual research.

Step 2

Personalised Messaging

Every message is written using real details about the prospect. Their company, their role, recent news, what they care about. It feels personal because it's based on real context.

Step 3

Multi Channel Outreach

Sequences run across email and LinkedIn at the same time. More chances to start a conversation, without your team juggling the outreach manually.

Step 4

Intelligent Follow Ups

Follow ups happen based on what the prospect actually does. Opened your email? Clicked a link? Gone quiet? Each situation gets the right next message at the right time.

Step 5

Meeting Booking

When someone's interested, they get sent straight to your calendar to pick a time. No back and forth. Just confirmed meetings in your diary.

Step 6

Reporting & Optimisation

You get full visibility on open rates, reply rates, meetings booked, and pipeline value. We keep refining the whole setup so results improve over time.

How Sales Automation Works

What the automation is actually doing behind the scenes

A proper sales automation system isn't just scheduled emails. It connects your lead sources, outreach tools, CRM, calendar, and reporting so everything keeps moving without your team updating five different systems.

That means leads get pulled in automatically, enriched with company data, written to your CRM, contacted with tailored messaging, scored on engagement, and handed to your team only when they're worth speaking to.

  • Lead sourcing pulls prospects from LinkedIn, website scrapers, forms, CRM lists, and databases.
  • Enrichment adds role, company, website, and sales context before outreach kicks off.
  • Outreach logic decides what to send, when to send it, and where to follow up.
  • CRM updates happen on their own so the pipeline stays clean and current.
  • Scoring and routing pushes warm replies to the right salesperson at the right time.
  • Dashboards and reporting show what's working, where replies are coming from, and where revenue's building.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is sales automation?+

Sales automation uses AI and workflow tools to handle repetitive sales tasks automatically. This includes finding prospects, researching companies, writing personalised outreach emails, sending follow-ups, booking meetings, and updating your CRM — all without your sales team doing it manually.

Will automated outreach damage our brand?+

No. We use AI to research each prospect individually and write genuinely personalised messages based on real data about their company, role, and industry. The emails feel human because they're informed by real context, not generic templates.

How many leads can sales automation generate?+

It depends on your target market and messaging, but most clients see a 3-5x increase in outreach volume while maintaining or improving reply rates. The key is quality personalisation at scale.

Does sales automation replace our sales team?+

No. It frees your sales team from the repetitive work so they can focus on what they're best at: having conversations and closing deals. The automation handles everything up to the point where a human conversation is needed.

What CRMs does your sales automation integrate with?+

We integrate with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, and most other major CRMs. Every interaction — opens, clicks, replies, and bookings — gets logged automatically so your pipeline data is always accurate.
