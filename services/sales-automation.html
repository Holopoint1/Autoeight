How It Works

A fully automated sales machine, built for you

From prospect discovery to booked meeting, every step of the outreach process is handled by intelligent automation. Your team focuses entirely on closing.

Step 1

Prospect Discovery

AI identifies and qualifies leads matching your ideal customer profile from multiple data sources, continuously feeding your pipeline with fresh prospects.

Step 2

Personalised Messaging

Every message is personalised using prospect data — company name, role, recent news, pain points — so it feels handwritten, even at scale.

Step 3

Multi-Channel Outreach

Sequences run across email and LinkedIn simultaneously, maximising touchpoints and increasing the chance of a response.

Step 4

Intelligent Follow-Ups

Automated follow-up sequences triggered by behaviour — opens, clicks, no response — with the right message at the right time.

Step 5

Meeting Booking

Interested prospects are guided to book directly in your calendar. No back and forth — just confirmed meetings in your diary.

Step 6

Reporting & Optimisation

Full visibility on open rates, reply rates, meetings booked, and pipeline value. We continuously optimise to improve performance.
