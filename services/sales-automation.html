How It Works

How we build your sales pipeline

From prospect discovery to booked meeting, every step of the outreach process is handled by intelligent automation. Your team focuses entirely on closing.

Step 1

Prospect Discovery

We set up systems that find and qualify leads matching your ideal customer profile. Fresh prospects get added to your pipeline every day without anyone doing manual research.

Step 2

Personalised Messaging

Every message gets written using real details about the prospect. Their company, role, recent news, what they care about. It reads like it was written just for them, even though the system handles hundreds a day.

Step 3

Multi-Channel Outreach

Sequences run across email and LinkedIn at the same time, hitting more touchpoints and giving you a much better chance of getting a reply.

Step 4

Intelligent Follow-Ups

The system sends follow-ups based on what the prospect actually does. Opened your email? Clicked a link? No response at all? Each one gets a different message at the right time.

Step 5

Meeting Booking

When someone's interested, they get sent straight to your calendar to pick a time. No back and forth emails. Just confirmed meetings in your diary.

Step 6

Reporting & Optimisation

You get full visibility on open rates, reply rates, meetings booked, and pipeline value. We keep optimising the whole thing to improve results over time.
