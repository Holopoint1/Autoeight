What is automated data reporting?

Automated data reporting pulls information from all your business tools, combines it into unified dashboards, and generates reports without manual effort. Instead of your team spending hours exporting data from different platforms and building spreadsheets, automated reporting delivers live dashboards and scheduled reports that update in real time. AI can also analyse trends, flag anomalies, and surface insights your team might miss.

Automated data reporting is for any business that makes decisions based on data from multiple sources. It's particularly valuable for companies with sales, marketing, and operations teams that each use different tools but need a unified view of performance.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is automated data reporting?+

Automated data reporting uses integrations and AI to pull data from all your business tools, combine it into a single view, and generate reports automatically. Instead of spending hours building spreadsheets, you get live dashboards and scheduled reports delivered to your inbox.

What kind of reports can you automate?+

Sales performance, marketing ROI, pipeline forecasts, customer metrics, operational KPIs, financial summaries. Essentially any report your team currently builds manually from data in your tools.

Do we need to change our current tools to use automated reporting?+

No. We pull data from the tools you already use. Google Sheets, your CRM, email platforms, ad accounts, accounting software. We connect to all of them and bring the data together in one place.

How accurate is automated reporting compared to manual reports?+

More accurate. Manual reports are prone to copy-paste errors, outdated data, and inconsistent formatting. Automated reports pull data directly from the source in real time, so the numbers are always current and correct.

Can I customise what's shown in my dashboards?+

Yes. We build dashboards around the metrics that matter to your business. You choose what you want to see, how it's grouped, and who gets access. Everything is tailored to how your team makes decisions.
