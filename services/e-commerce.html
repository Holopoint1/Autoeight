FAQ

Common questions from specialist retailers.

Why should I leave Etsy or a marketplace?

Etsy takes 6.5% per sale plus 4% + 20p payment processing plus listing fees on every product. If you turn over more than about £10k a year they also opt you into offsite ads which can add up to 15% on top. Every marketplace has similar economics. On your own platform, that money stays with you. Own site pays back the build within months for anyone doing consistent sales.

What kind of retailers do you build for?

UK specialist retailers, indie makers, and owner-operated brands: laser-cut and craft businesses, artisan food, specialty homeware, hobby goods, kits, custom apparel, subscription boxes, direct-to-consumer brands. If you sell your own products online, we build the platform for it.

Do I lose SEO rankings if I leave Etsy?

You never had SEO on Etsy. Etsy ranks. You didn't. On your own site we build in local and specialty search from day one, so Google search + AI chats like ChatGPT and Perplexity name your brand when someone searches for what you sell.

What about payments and customer emails?

Direct Stripe integration means every payment lands in your bank account minus only Stripe's 1.4% + 20p. Every customer email is yours, so you can send order confirmations, dispatch updates, follow-ups and review requests in your brand voice, not a marketplace's template.

Can I still use marketplaces alongside my own site?

Yes. Most specialist retailers we work with keep a small marketplace presence for discovery and push all repeat customers to their own site. We can integrate the stock and orders so you're not managing two inventories.

How much does it cost?

Custom e-commerce builds start around £3,500 for a focused platform. Fixed quote per project, no per-transaction fees on top. Rough budget at the end of a free 30-minute call.
