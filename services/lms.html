What is a Learning Management System?

A Learning Management System (LMS) is a platform that hosts your courses, tracks who has completed what, and gives students, teachers, parents, or staff one place to learn. It replaces scattered PDFs, shared drives, email chains, and spreadsheets with a single branded hub where content lives, progress is tracked, assignments are submitted, and work gets marked.

A custom LMS is for anyone teaching in a repeatable way — schools running curriculum, colleges delivering qualifications, tutors running after-school sessions, training providers delivering accredited courses, and companies onboarding staff or running compliance and product training. Unlike Moodle, Google Classroom, or Canvas, a custom platform is shaped around exactly how you work, not how the average customer works.

How It Works

How we build your learning platform

We start with what you're teaching and how you teach it. Then we build the platform around that — not the other way round.

1

Discovery & structure

We sit down with whoever runs the learning — head of department, training lead, tutor, or L&D manager — and map out your courses, roles, and how progress actually needs to be tracked.

2

Content & course import

We pull in whatever you already have. Word docs, PDFs, PowerPoints, YouTube videos, SCORM packages, worksheet libraries. Nothing gets left behind, and nothing needs re-writing from scratch.

3

Build & brand

We build the platform around your courses and users. Student portals, teacher dashboards, parent access, admin tools, assessments, certificates — whatever your setup needs. Fully branded, on your own domain.

4

Launch & improve

We train your team, onboard your first cohort, and stick around to refine things based on how it's actually being used. New courses, new features, new integrations — the platform grows with you.

Built for anyone teaching anything

One home for every class, student, and parent

Everything you'd expect from a serious LMS, built around you

Student portal

Secure logins, a clean dashboard of current courses and assignments, progress bars, due dates, and everywhere they need to be in one click.

Parent & guardian access

Teacher & admin dashboards

Assignments & submissions

Upload work, submit online, attach files, add comments. Everything tied back to the student, the course, and the deadline so nothing gets lost.

Progress tracking & achievements

Multi-role auth & permissions

Students, parents, teachers, admins, external examiners — each role sees exactly what they should, and nothing they shouldn't. Built with proper security from day one.

Integrations with the tools you already use

HR systems for onboarding, SIS / MIS for schools, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Stripe for paid courses, Zoom and Google Meet for live lessons. Your LMS plugs into whatever you've already got.

Custom vs Off-the-Shelf

Why custom beats Moodle, Canvas, or Google Classroom

Generic LMS platforms are built for the average customer. If your setup is specific — and it always is — you end up fighting the tool instead of using it.

Built around your workflow

No paying for modules you'll never use or fighting a menu system designed for someone else's school. Every screen in your LMS exists because you need it.

No per-user licences

Off-the-shelf LMS platforms charge per seat, per month, forever. A custom build is a one-off project with ongoing hosting that scales cheaply — not linearly with your user count.

Your brand, your domain

Students and parents log into your platform, not a third-party site with your logo bolted on. Makes you look like the professional operation you are.

Our Stack

The tools we use to build learning platforms

We pick the stack to match the job. Here's what usually goes into a custom LMS build.

Supabase

Postgres database, multi-role auth, row-level security, and file storage. The backbone of every platform we build. GDPR-friendly, hosted in the EU.

Cloudflare

Global CDN, DDoS protection, and Workers for serverless functions. Your LMS stays fast and secure whether you've got 30 students or 30,000.

SCORM 1.2 / 2004

The industry standard for packaging learning content. We build to it so your courses can be exported, reused, or handed to awarding bodies when needed.

Python & JavaScript

Custom Python scripts for course generation from Word docs, and JavaScript for everything the student sees in the browser. Fast, maintainable, no bloat.

Stripe

For paid courses, memberships, subscriptions, or per-seat billing. Plugs straight into user access so paying students get instant access.

OpenAI & Claude

For anything AI-powered — auto-generating quizzes from source material, marking free-text responses, answering student questions, or summarising lessons.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is a Learning Management System?+

A Learning Management System (LMS) is a platform that hosts your courses, tracks who has completed what, and gives students, teachers, parents, or staff one place to learn. It replaces scattered PDFs, emails, spreadsheets, and video links with a single branded hub where content lives, progress is tracked, and work gets marked.

Who is a custom LMS for?+

Schools, colleges, private tutors, training providers, and any company that needs to onboard staff or run internal training. If you're teaching people something in a repeatable way — whether that's GCSE maths, compliance training, product onboarding, or apprentice curriculum — a custom LMS gives you a proper home for it.

Why build a custom LMS instead of using Moodle or Google Classroom?+

Off-the-shelf systems like Moodle, Google Classroom, and Canvas are built for the average user and get in your way the moment you want anything specific. A custom LMS is built around your exact workflow — your branding, your course structure, your user roles, your reporting — with no licence fees per user and no features you'll never use.

Can you import existing courses, worksheets, or SCORM packages?+

Yes. We've built course builders that read straight from Word documents and generate the LMS pages automatically. We handle SCORM packages, PDFs, PowerPoints, YouTube embeds, and interactive worksheets. If your content exists, we can get it in.

How long does it take to build a custom LMS?+

A focused LMS with student portal, progress tracking, and course content is usually live in 4-8 weeks. Larger platforms with parent access, teacher dashboards, admin tools, assessments, and custom integrations take 8-16 weeks. We phase the build so you get something usable early, then layer features on top.
