What is CRM integration?

CRM integration is the process of connecting your customer relationship management system to all your other business tools so that data flows between them automatically. This eliminates manual data entry, prevents duplicate records, and creates a single source of truth for every customer interaction. When your CRM is properly integrated, every email, call, form submission, and purchase updates your customer records in real time.

CRM integration is essential for any business that uses a CRM alongside other tools like email platforms, accounting software, marketing tools, or project management systems. If your team spends time manually updating records or switching between tools to find information, integration will save them hours every week.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is CRM integration?+

CRM integration connects your customer relationship management system to all your other business tools so data flows between them automatically. This means no more manual data entry, no duplicate records, and a single source of truth for every customer interaction.

Which CRMs do you work with?+

We work with HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive, Zoho, Monday.com, and most other major CRMs. If your CRM has an API, we can integrate it.

Can you migrate data from our old CRM?+

Yes. We handle full CRM migrations including data cleaning, deduplication, field mapping, and validation. We make sure nothing gets lost and everything lands in the right place.

How long does CRM integration take?+

Basic integrations can be live within 1-2 weeks. More complex setups with multiple data sources, custom fields, and automation workflows typically take 2-4 weeks.

Will CRM integration work with our existing workflows?+

Yes. We build integrations that enhance your existing processes, not replace them. Data syncs happen in the background so your team keeps working the way they're used to, just without the manual data entry.
