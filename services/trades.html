The Problem

Word of mouth was enough. It isn't anymore.

Most UK trade businesses grew on word of mouth, referrals, and Checkatrade. That worked when customers were older, less online, and picked up the phonebook. It doesn't work as well now that every quote starts with a Google search or an AI chat.

Customers now type "joiner near me" into Google or ask ChatGPT "who's a good joiner in Halifax?". If your site isn't set up properly to rank on both, the enquiries go to the trade next door with a better website. Not a better tradesperson. A better website.

FAQ

Common questions from trade owners.

What kind of trade businesses do you build websites for?

Joiners, carpenters, builders, plumbers, electricians, roofers, plasterers, tilers, kitchen fitters, bathroom fitters, and any UK trade business that gets work from local Google searches. If your customers find you online, we can build the site that gets them to you.

How does the site actually bring in leads?

Automated quote request forms wired straight to your phone or inbox. When someone fills in the form on the site, you get a text or email within seconds with their name, phone number, and job details. No missed enquiries, no forms sitting in a WordPress admin nobody checks.

Will it rank in the towns I work in?

That's the whole point. We set up local SEO across every service area you cover, register you properly with Google Business Profile, and structure the site so search engines and AI chats like ChatGPT and Perplexity understand which towns you serve. Not something bolted on later. Built in from day one.

Do I need to manage the site myself?

No. We build it, launch it, and monitor it. If you want to add a gallery photo or update your services, we can wire in a simple admin so you can. Otherwise we handle it. Optional monthly support keeps the site running and improving.
