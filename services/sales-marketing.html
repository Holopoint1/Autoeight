What is sales and marketing intelligence?

Sales and marketing intelligence is the automated collection, analysis, and delivery of data about your prospects, competitors, and market. AI-powered intelligence tools monitor competitor activity, track industry trends, enrich your CRM data with real-time information, and surface buying signals that help your team prioritise the right opportunities. It replaces hours of manual research with automated insights delivered directly to your team.

Sales and marketing intelligence is most valuable for B2B companies with active sales teams, marketing departments that need data-driven campaign decisions, and any business that wants to understand its market without spending hours on manual research.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is sales and marketing intelligence?+

Sales and marketing intelligence uses AI to automatically gather, analyse, and act on data about your prospects, competitors, and market. It replaces manual research with automated insights that help your team make better decisions faster.

How does AI help with market research?+

AI can monitor competitor pricing, track industry news, analyse customer sentiment, and identify buying signals across the web, all automatically. Instead of spending hours on research, your team gets actionable intelligence delivered to them.

Can this integrate with our existing marketing tools?+

Yes. We connect with HubSpot, Mailchimp, Google Analytics, LinkedIn, and most other marketing platforms. Data flows between your tools automatically so everything stays in sync.

How is this different from a regular CRM?+

A CRM stores data your team enters manually. Sales and marketing intelligence actively gathers new data, enriches your existing records, and surfaces insights your team would never find on their own. It makes your CRM smarter.

What kind of businesses benefit most from this?+

B2B companies with active sales teams see the biggest impact. If your team spends time researching prospects, tracking competitors, or trying to understand what content drives leads, this will save them hours every week.
