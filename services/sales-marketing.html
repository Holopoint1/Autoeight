Use Cases

Tools that give your team a real edge

Your CRM has the data. We turn it into dashboards, maps, forecasts, and intel feeds your team will actually use every day.

Dashboards

Sales Dashboards

Real-time sales intelligence pulled straight from your CRM. HubSpot, Salesforce, Pipedrive — we connect directly so your dashboards update live every time a deal moves, a call gets logged, or a quote gets sent.

  • Live integration with HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive — data pulls automatically
  • KPIs update in real time — no manual exports or spreadsheet refreshes
  • Role-based views so your CEO sees the pipeline, reps see their targets
  • Connects to Google Sheets, Xero, or your data warehouse if you need deeper reporting
Forecasting

Pipeline Forecasting

Your CRM deals feed directly into weighted forecasts. We pull every deal stage, probability, and close date from HubSpot or Salesforce so your leadership team gets a revenue view they can actually trust — updated in real time, not once a month.

  • Pulls deal data live from HubSpot, Salesforce, or Pipedrive
  • Weighted pipeline by probability, stage, and expected close date
  • Rep-level and team-level forecast breakdowns
  • Historical win-rate analysis so you can forecast based on real patterns
Market Intel

Marketing Trends

We pull your sales data from HubSpot or Salesforce and cross-reference it with market signals, competitor activity, and seasonal patterns. Your team sees what's trending before your competitors do.

  • Hot product detection using live CRM sales data
  • Seasonal analysis based on your actual deal history
  • Competitor intelligence from RSS feeds, news alerts, and web monitoring
  • Campaign correlation — see which marketing efforts drive actual pipeline
Territory

Territory Mapper

We pull your customer locations from your CRM and plot them on an interactive map. See where your deals are, where your gaps are, and plan the most efficient route for field visits — all driven by live CRM data.

  • Accounts pulled from HubSpot/Salesforce and plotted on a live map
  • Colour-coded pins by deal stage, value, rep, or last contact date
  • Route optimisation for field visits using Google Maps integration
  • Coverage gap analysis — see where you're not reaching
Training

Sales Training & Onboarding Hub

Everything your sales team needs to sell effectively, in one place. New starters follow a structured onboarding path and get up to speed in days instead of weeks. Experienced reps get live competitor analysis, battle cards, and product guides that stay current.

  • Structured onboarding for new hires — learning paths, quizzes, and sign-off tracking
  • Competitor analysis sheets that update as you gather new intel
  • Battle cards for every competitor and common objection
  • Product guides, pricing calculators, and demo scripts in one searchable hub
  • Manager visibility into who's completed training and who needs support
  • Video library for walkthroughs, call recordings, and best-practice examples
Prospect Intel

Lead News Feed

Know everything about a prospect before you pick up the phone. AI-curated news, company updates, and trigger events so your reps always have a reason to call.

  • Trigger alerts when something relevant happens
