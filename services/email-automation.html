Email Automation
Your inbox, handled

Whether you need fully hands-off email handling or AI assisted drafts your team reviews before sending, we build the right level of automation for your business.

Get started
Two approaches

Choose the level of automation that's right for you

Both options are powered by the same AI core and the same detailed customer datasheet — the difference is how much human involvement you want.

Full Automation

Fully Automated Customer Service Email

The AI reads every incoming email, understands the context, pulls the right information from your customer datasheet, crafts a reply, and sends it — completely autonomously. No human involved.

Ideal for businesses receiving high volumes of routine enquiries where speed and scale matter most.

  • Instant responses, 24/7
  • Handles unlimited volume
  • Answers grounded in your datasheet
  • Smart escalation to humans when needed
Human-in-the-Loop

AI Draft, Human Review & Send

For teams where integrity and accuracy are paramount. The AI drafts a complete, context-aware response — but a human reviews it and presses send. You stay in control, the AI does the heavy lifting.

Ideal for businesses handling complex, sensitive, or high-value client communications.

  • Full human oversight before sending
  • Dramatically faster drafting
  • Consistent tone and accuracy
  • Your team edits before it goes out

Integrations

Every tool your team already uses, connected

Our email automation plugs directly into your existing tech stack — no migration, no disruption. Each integration is configured to match exactly how your business operates.

Click any integration below to see how it connects and what it can do for your workflow.

We connect via secure OAuth — no passwords stored. The AI reads incoming emails, applies your label and folder logic, and sends replies directly from your address. Works with shared inboxes, aliases, and Google Groups out of the box.

Full integration via the Microsoft Graph API. Handles shared mailboxes, distribution lists, and delegated access. Meeting requests, calendar events, and auto-replies are all within scope — no third-party add-ins required.

Every email — sent and received — is automatically logged against the right contact and deal. Deal stages update based on reply content, new contacts are created for unknown senders, and your pipeline stays clean with zero manual data entry.

Email events fire webhooks into Zapier or Make, triggering actions across your full stack — Slack alerts, Notion entries, invoice creation, or anything else your workflows need. Inbound emails can also trigger outbound sequences automatically.

The Foundation

Everything the AI knows about your business lives in one datasheet

Before we go live, we work with you to build a detailed customer datasheet — a structured document containing everything the AI needs to represent your business accurately. Pricing, products, policies, FAQs, tone of voice, escalation rules, and more.

The AI only responds based on what's in this datasheet. It never guesses, never improvises, and never goes off-brand. If it doesn't know the answer, it escalates correctly.

Accuracy Guaranteed

Responses are always grounded in your approved content — no hallucinations, no off-script answers.

Your Voice, Your Tone

We train the AI to match your brand's communication style, so every reply sounds like it came from your team.

Always Up to Date

Update the datasheet and the AI's knowledge updates instantly — no retraining needed.

Smart Escalation

Anything outside the datasheet scope is flagged and routed to the right human, with full context.
