Two approaches

Pick the level of automation that works for your team

Both options use the same AI engine and the same customer datasheet. The only difference is how much control you want your team to keep.

Full Automation

Fully Automated Customer Service Email

The AI reads every incoming email, works out what the person needs, pulls the right info from your customer datasheet, writes the reply, and sends it for you.

Great for businesses that get a high volume of routine enquiries where speed matters and response times need to stay sharp.

  • Instant responses, 24/7
  • Handles unlimited volume
  • Answers grounded in your datasheet
  • Smart escalation to humans when needed
Draft Mode

AI Drafts, Your Team Sends

For teams that want to move faster without giving up control. The AI writes a full reply with all the context, then drops it into drafts for your team to review and send.

This works really well inside Gmail, Outlook, and shared inbox tools. You can turn draft mode on or off whenever you want.

  • Full human oversight before sending
  • Way faster reply writing
  • Consistent tone based on your company's knowledge
  • Your team can edit, approve, or send in seconds

Integrations

Plugs into the tools your team already uses

Our email automation connects straight to your existing tools. No migration, no disruption. Each integration is set up around how your business already works.

Click any integration below to see how it connects and what it can do for your workflow.

We connect through secure OAuth, no passwords stored. The AI reads incoming emails, applies your label and folder logic, and either sends replies or drops them into drafts from your address. It works with shared inboxes, aliases, and Google Groups straight away.

Full integration through the Microsoft Graph API. Handles shared mailboxes, distribution lists, and delegated access. Meeting requests, calendar events, and ready-made draft replies all fit into the same setup. No extra add-ins needed.

Every email, sent and received, gets logged against the right contact and deal. Deal stages update based on reply content, new contacts get created for unknown senders, and your pipeline stays tidy without anyone doing data entry.

Email events can trigger actions across your full stack. Slack alerts, Notion entries, invoices, handovers for your team. Inbound emails can also kick off follow-up sequences automatically when it makes sense for your workflow.
