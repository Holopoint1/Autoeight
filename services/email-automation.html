The Problem

Your team is spending hours on stuff AI can handle in seconds

Most support teams are buried in the same questions every day. Where's my order, can I get a refund, how do I change my password. Your team knows the answer, but finding the info and typing the reply still takes time.

Slow responses

Customers wait hours for answers to "where's my order?" while your team manually looks things up across different systems.

Rising costs

Every ticket handled manually costs time and money. As volume grows, you either hire more people or let response times slip.

No context

Generic chatbots don't know your products, pricing, or policies. Without your actual data, they just give vague answers that frustrate people.

Can't take action

Most tools can only reply. They can't actually cancel an order, process a refund, or check stock. Your team still has to do that bit manually.

The Solution

AI that actually knows your business and can take action

We connect your AI to your real data — orders, products, pricing, policies, CRM. It doesn't just reply. It looks things up, processes requests, and handles tickets from start to finish.

Instant answers using real-time order, shipping, and product data
Automated actions like refunds, cancellations, and order updates
One system instead of switching between 8 browser tabs
24/7 support without scaling your team or adding night shifts

Two approaches

Pick the level of automation that works for your team

Both options use the same AI engine and the same customer datasheet. The only difference is how much control you want your team to keep.

Full Automation

Fully Automated Customer Service

AI handles emails, chats, and tickets automatically. It reads every incoming message, works out what the customer needs, pulls the right info from your datasheet, and responds across whichever channel they used.

Great for businesses that get a high volume of routine enquiries across multiple channels where speed and consistency matter most.

  • Instant responses across all channels, 24/7
  • Handles unlimited volume
  • Answers grounded in your datasheet
  • Smart escalation to humans when needed
Draft Mode

AI Drafts, Your Team Sends

For teams that want to move faster without giving up control. AI prepares responses across all channels — emails, chat replies, ticket responses — then queues them for your team to review and send.

Works inside Gmail, Outlook, your chat platform, and your ticketing system. You can turn draft mode on or off per channel whenever you want.

  • Full human oversight before anything goes out
  • Way faster response times across every channel
  • Consistent tone based on your company's knowledge
  • Your team can edit, approve, or send in seconds

Integrations

Plugs into the tools your team already uses

Our email automation connects straight to your existing tools. No migration, no disruption. Each integration is set up around how your business already works.

Click any integration below to see how it connects and what it can do for your workflow.

We connect through secure OAuth, no passwords stored. The AI reads incoming emails, applies your label and folder logic, and either sends replies or drops them into drafts from your address. It works with shared inboxes, aliases, and Google Groups straight away.

Full integration through the Microsoft Graph API. Handles shared mailboxes, distribution lists, and delegated access. Meeting requests, calendar events, and ready-made draft replies all fit into the same setup. No extra add-ins needed.

Every email, sent and received, gets logged against the right contact and deal. Deal stages update based on reply content, new contacts get created for unknown senders, and your pipeline stays tidy without anyone doing data entry.

Email events can trigger actions across your full stack. Slack alerts, Notion entries, invoices, handovers for your team. Inbound emails can also kick off follow-up sequences automatically when it makes sense for your workflow.
