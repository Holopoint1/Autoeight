What are internal automation systems?

Internal automation systems connect your business tools and processes so that data, tasks, and communications flow between them automatically. This includes employee onboarding workflows, approval chains, document management, internal reporting, team notifications, and any repetitive administrative process that currently requires manual effort. Automation removes the need for your team to copy data between tools, chase approvals, or generate routine reports by hand.

Internal automation is for any business that runs on multiple tools and has team members spending significant time on administrative tasks. It's especially impactful for growing companies where manual processes are starting to become bottlenecks.

FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What are internal automation systems?+

Internal automation systems connect your business tools and processes so data flows between them automatically. This includes things like onboarding workflows, approval chains, document management, internal reporting, and team notifications, all running without manual effort.

Can you automate our onboarding process?+

Yes. We can automate employee and client onboarding from end to end. Document collection, account setup, training assignments, welcome emails, and status tracking can all run automatically when triggered.

How do internal automations save money?+

By removing the hours your team spends on repetitive admin tasks. Data entry, status updates, report generation, file management. These tasks add up to thousands of hours per year across most businesses. Automating them costs a fraction of the staff time they replace.

Will this disrupt our current workflow?+

No. We build automations that plug into your existing tools and processes. We run everything in parallel first so you can verify it works before switching over. There's no big-bang migration.

What tools can you connect for internal systems?+

We integrate with Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Slack, Notion, Asana, Trello, Airtable, and hundreds of other business tools. If it has an API or works with Zapier/Make, we can connect it.
